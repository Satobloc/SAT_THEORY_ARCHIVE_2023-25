Greetings and salutations! Welcome to the third installment of the floating
liars’club. Things are progressing well, although we are all extraordinarily
busy. Between writing, editing, and workshopping stories, and doinglayoy,
artwork, marketing, and promotion for the magazine, we are left litle time a
eat or sleep.

We have received a lot of positive feedback from our readers, and the
submissions have started to flow in, including interest from as far away as the
U.K. We encourage anyone interested in writing to submit a piece of work
While we cannot publish everything we read, we will certainly provide feedback
to anyone who requests it. While we are primarily interested in short fiction,
weare also willing to look at poetry, creative nonfiction, and artwork. Submit!

This issue again features the fantastic artwork and illustration of Joe Bellomy,
highlighting an interesting array of stories. While we have no intention of
theme from issue to issue, this month’s stories seem to congeal together nicely,
Many of the stories contain the theme of discovery or uncovering things, ether
in the figurative sense of characters uncovering things about les, as in
Ben Crites’ “The Nature of Fireflies,” or in the very literal sense as in my own
story, “A Buried Mystery.”

The process of writing is one of constant uncovering and discovery. Both of
the stories I included in this issue were inspired by short stories I had recently
discovered, namely Jorge Luis Borges’ “A Secret Miracle” and J.G. Ballard’s

hem:

“The Drowned Giant.” This process of discovery and uncovering comes from
everywhere, inside and out, Part of the process of learning to be a writer is
being aware of the things you are uncovering daily.
