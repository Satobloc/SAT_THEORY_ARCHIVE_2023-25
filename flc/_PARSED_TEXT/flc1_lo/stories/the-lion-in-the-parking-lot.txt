Arnold didn’t know. He did know the doors of
the office were auto locked at precisely five p.m. and
unless the cat took someone else soon he'd be running
for it with all the other schmucks. He thought a bit of
overtime might give him an edge. Maybe the cat would
snag another while he was still exiting the building.
However, the delay may have worked against him. The
crowd was thinning now and the quickest employees
had already made it to their vehicles and were speeding
off for home.

He glanced at his watch. In moments when the
doors locked he’d have to run for it anyway. So he tucked
his briefcase up under his arm and made a dash for the
first row of parking spaces.

There was a time when he'd been the first out
of the building. Almost daring the cat to make a go for
