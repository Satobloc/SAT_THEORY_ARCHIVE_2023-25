Well, there’re bumblebees and sage
and ponds and kids swimming.
There’re broken monitors and spare
parts and grease and rusty, knuckle-
breaking bolts and bookstores and
endless cups of coffee. Cigarettes,
stained glass, dresses, holes-in-one,
and Serena’s skintight, black tennis
uniform; there’s a lost wrench, a lost
sock, lost key, infinite shades of green.
There’s misspelling and curses and
witches, hookers, businessmen, horse
trainers, candy shop owners and evil
clowns. There are secrets. Some things
never said. Impossible to say. Vocabu-
lary. Voice boxes. Muscles, punctua-
tion, Marilyn Monroe, Caesar, Jesus
and Groucho Marx. There is a Charo
and a painted stone sitting in a flower
pot. An ashtray, cicadas. There’s a guy
I know...a thought I had...a dream I
dreamt...

And then there’s fiction.
