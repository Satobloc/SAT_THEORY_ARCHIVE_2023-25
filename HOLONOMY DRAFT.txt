## 3 Background Dynamics

For a spatially flat FRW metric, the slow‑roll equations are

$$
3H^2 M_P^2 &\simeq V(θ),\\
3H\dot θ &\simeq −V'(θ) = −(Λ^4 ⁄ f )\sin(θ ⁄ f ).
\end{aligned}\]

Define ε and η:
\[ε = \tfrac12 (V'/V)^2 (M_P^2), \quad η = (V'' ⁄ V) M_P^2.\]

Inflation ends when **max(ε,|η|) ≈ 1**.
