SAT PREDICTIONS 3

Parameterized SAT Predictions – Public Version v1.0
Date: 2025-05-08 Time of Lock-In: 23:41 UTC Framework: Scalar Angular Topology (SAT) – unreleased

1. Gravitational Wave Propagation Anomalies – LISA
	•	Parameter: Residual phase delay Δφ vs. sky angle θ
	•	Expected Deviation: Δφ ~ 10⁻³–10⁻² radians over baseline depending on θ
	•	Falsifiability Threshold: No directional dependence in residuals after subtracting redshift and known lensing effects

2. Polarization/Chirp Bias in FRBs – SKA
	•	Parameter: Chirp slope δ(f)/δt and polarization angle ψ(θ)
	•	Expected Anomaly: ψ(θ) shows weak dipole (~few degrees systematic offset) over sky sectors; chirp slope shows 1–2% deviation from dispersion-model fit in angularly aligned events
	•	Falsifiability Threshold: All residuals align with IGM/ISM plasma or magnetic field models alone

3. Residual Weak Lensing Anisotropy – Euclid / Roman
	•	Parameter: B-mode shear power C_B(ℓ), ellipticity residual δe(θ)
	•	Expected Anomaly: B-mode excess at low multipoles ℓ = 2–20; δe(θ) ~ 10⁻³–10⁻² systematic bias aligned with celestial coordinates
	•	Falsifiability Threshold: Residuals match ΛCDM + intrinsic alignment + known systematics

4. Orbital Drift in TNOs – Rubin
	•	Parameter: Time derivative of orbital elements dω/dt, di/dt
	•	Expected Drift: Deviations of ~10⁻⁴ deg/year in perihelion longitude ω or inclination i, with directional clustering
	•	Falsifiability Threshold: All drift accounted for by planetary perturbations, radiation pressure, or measurement error

5. Tidal Stream Curvature Anomalies – Rubin
	•	Parameter: Angular deviation Δα from modeled stream path
	•	Expected Residual: Δα ~ 0.05°–0.2°, systematic by sky sector
	•	Falsifiability Threshold: All curvature explainable via N-body subhalo interactions

6. Solar System Dust Photometry Asymmetry – Rubin
	•	Parameter: Δmagnitude = m_north – m_south, polarization vector Δψ
	•	Expected Asymmetry: Δmagnitude ~ 0.1–0.3 mag over latitude bands; polarization asymmetry Δψ ~ 2°–5°
	•	Falsifiability Threshold: All asymmetries correlate with solar phase angle, latitude-dependent scattering geometry

7. Angular Memory in Repeating Transients – Rubin
	•	Parameter: Residual in recurrence interval ΔT(z, θ)
	•	Expected Offset: ΔT ~ 1–5% deviation from expected time-dilation scaling; statistically correlated with RA/Dec
	•	Falsifiability Threshold: No significant θ-dependent drift across redshift-matched populations

8. Microlensing Parameter Drift – Roman
	•	Parameter: Einstein crossing time t_E(θ), inferred lens mass M_lens(θ)
	•	Expected Effect: Systematic directional offset in mean t_E of ~1–3% across opposing sky sectors
	•	Falsifiability Threshold: All residuals map to stellar density, lens distribution, and parallax

9. Directional Neutrino Oscillation Variation – DUNE / Hyper-K
	•	Parameter: Δm²_eff(θ), comparing up/down, crust/mantle paths
	•	Expected Shift: Δ(Δm²) ~ 10⁻⁵–10⁻⁴ eV² between paths of same baseline but different orientation
	•	Falsifiability Threshold: All oscillation spectra match matter effect models without orientation correlation

10. Atom Interferometry Orientation Phase Shift – MAGIS / AION
	•	Parameter: Interference phase shift Δφ(α, β, γ), where (α, β, γ) = instrument Euler angles
	•	Expected Effect: Δφ ~ 10⁻³ radians when interferometer is rotated through specific sky-fixed alignments
	•	Falsifiability Threshold: All phase shifts symmetric under spatial rotation and accounted for by local fields
