Notes: The OCR is poor to moderate, the ordering is somewhat scrambled, but generally, | ### | is the divider between episodes meta data. Unless otherwise obviously not true, anything between two dividers is a metadata about a single episode. Note that retention is measured only during the first week, total listens include all weeks, and retention rates for anyone who listened after the first week must be estimated based on total listening duration for the entire time the episode has been out, divided by the total number of streams and downloads properly. Any episode with a date but an unspecified year should be assumed to be 2026 unless the context suggests that the absence of a year is clearly a corruption. In the episode that contains the word filament (or Filament…) is about SAT, the Scalar-Angular Torsion (sometimes ‘Twist’) Theory, or just (the) Scalar-Angular theory/framework, or its pre-formalization schematic version, originally  nicknamed  “Stringing-Along Theory”. Other names for the theory have been floated, occasionally or hallucinated by the LLM, but the official name is just SAT. Timestamps without dates are either episode chapter titles or total duration of the episode usually. Not all episodes have the same data, not all episodes present the data in the same order. ‘=====‘ usually  means division between analytic sections of an episodes, meta.

Contemplating the physical basis of consciousness. A note on SAT:
I call it a "toy theory" because it's not meant to be a serious
scientific theory. Perhaps it could be developed into one, but not
by me. Instead, "Stringing-Along Theory" is just trying to take
seriously what a four-dimensional universe might look like. It has
basically nothing to do with String Theory proper, which is why I
call the elementary structures "filaments" just to differentiate the
two. That said, the superficial similarities are obvious, so the Dee
Dive hosts can be forgiven for mixing them up a bit.

METRIC EMERGENCE FROM THE ENSEMBLE
