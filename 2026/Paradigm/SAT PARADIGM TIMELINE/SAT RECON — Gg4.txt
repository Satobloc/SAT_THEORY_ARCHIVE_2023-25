	•	The Process: Instead of adding speculative new objects like strings or extra dimensions to fix the math, physicists test whether the strength of gravity naturally hits a fixed, finite maximum limit as you zoom in toward the sub-Planck scale.

	•	The Process: Theorists completely ignore unobservable internal mechanisms (like trying to see inside a particle). Instead, they look strictly at the "S-Matrix"—the mathematical grid that maps what goes into a particle collision and what comes out.
	•	The Goal: By enforcing strict mathematical consistency, causality, and probability conservation onto the collision outputs, the allowed theories lock themselves into place. This eliminates human guesswork, as the math itself dictates the only viable paths to a TOE.
5. Algorithmic and Machine Learning Exploration
