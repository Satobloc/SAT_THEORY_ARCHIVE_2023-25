The two-force organization

The current theory is moving toward two fundamental interaction classes.

Electrogravity

Electrogravity is the long-range filament–medium sector. Helical or rotating worldtubes stir Kelvin-like circulation in the vacuum representation. At fine resolution, signed chirality and circulation are intended to recover electromagnetic behavior; at coarse resolution, chirality cancellation and collective vortex response are intended to recover gravity and the Einstein metric.

GR remains the default successful description of ordinary gravity. SAT is not supposed to throw it away. The task is to geometrize it, identify the underlying vortex/worldtube behavior, and determine whether anything remains beyond GR.

The dramatic weakness of macroscopic gravity is no longer supposed to be explained by a lattice attenuation coefficient. The current possibilities involve:

cancellation of opposite chiral contributions;
different scale ratios between coil, body, spacing, and medium;
collective entrainment;
mechanical impedance or network response;
possibly an auxiliary interfilamental history-tug.

The Kelvin-vortex picture is current conceptual direction, but the equations are not yet mature.

Interfilamental interaction

This is direct finite-core worldtube-on-worldtube mechanics:

braiding and confinement;
collision and contact;
resistance to separation;
higher-order analogues of strong binding;
possible asymptotic contact between event-horizon-like boundaries;
cross-temporal tug or retained shared-history tension.

The strong force is the principal microscopic case. The proposed gravitational “filament tug” is an auxiliary member of this same class, not automatically the main gravitational mechanism.

Vacuum and matter

The vacuum is tentatively modeled as a ground-state ensemble—possibly BEC-like—of relatively straight, minimally resistant histories. Matter corresponds to persistent finite-core coiling or closure structure.

Mass is not currently a fixed Q-formula. The surviving idea is:

Cosmological circulation and recycling

A major speculative branch treats black holes as continuation channels rather than terminal singularities. Worldtubes may have ER-bridge-like morphology; their finite-core boundary may behave analogously to an event horizon. A path through a black hole may return, through a Klein-bottle-like or other nonorientable global identification, to the Big-Bang boundary.

This gives a recycling picture:

collapse→black-hole sink→Big-Bang source/dark-energy return.

The Big Bang may therefore be continuously fed by collapsed history rather than being only a past event. Black-hole merger, partial braiding rather than complete merger, information retention, and dark-energy dumping are all active conjectures—not deductions.

The older claim that macroscopic black holes literally collapse into protons, mesons, or baryons is not something I regard as current established SAT. The more defensible surviving structure is a possible scale analogy between finite-core worldtube contact, black-hole horizons, braid formation, and exclusion boundaries.

Standard physics and the working method

The governing method is:

Assume standard physics is generally correct within its tested domain.
Choose a minimal system exhibiting the behavior and a nearly identical null comparator.
Calculate the conventional system as completely as possible.
map its full history into four-dimensional geometry;
remove the inherited labels;
describe what the geometry itself does;
reuse existing mathematical objects before inventing SAT-specific quantities;
add no mechanism merely because a numerical gap needs filling;
apply every admitted mechanism consistently across comparable systems;
promote an idea only when it predicts or resolves something outside the problem that motivated it.

This is perhaps the most important part of the rebuild. The project is not “replace physics with rope metaphors.” It is use standard physics to constrain the geometry, then test whether the same minimal geometric grammar unexpectedly unifies apparently different domains. The Architect document states essentially this workflow directly.
