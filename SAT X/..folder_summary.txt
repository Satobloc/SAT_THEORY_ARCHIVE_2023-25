DOCUMENT ACCESS: Full text read, lines 1-238.
THOROUGHNESS: Complete first-pass close reading; mathematical claims assessed conceptually but not independently formalized in Lean/symbolic algebra.

This file proposes a minimal 1+1D Hamiltonian for the SAT theta_4-psi/u sector. It begins with a reduced Mark IV.2 Lagrangian in natural units, involving theta_4, u^mu, and beta sin^2(theta_4) partial_mu u^mu, subject to u_mu u^mu = -1. It then solves the unit-timelike constraint by introducing psi(t,x) such that u^mu = (cosh psi, sinh psi). This converts the constrained vector sector into a scalar/rapidity field sector.

The document derives canonical momenta pi_theta = dot(theta_4) and pi_psi = 2 alpha cosh^2(psi) dot(psi), then presents a Hamiltonian density and small-fluctuation spectrum. It concludes that the system is ghost-free/tachyon-free for alpha > 0 and says the final expression is first-class, positive-definite, and ready for quantization or numerical simulation.

Recommended status: theory-building scaffold / candidate reduced Hamiltonian, not yet a trusted final simulation core.

Important audit flags:
- Possible Legendre-transform issue: from pi_psi = 2 alpha cosh^2(psi) dot(psi), the Hamiltonian kinetic term would normally scale like pi_psi^2 / (4 alpha cosh^2(psi)), whereas the document writes a term proportional to 4 alpha cosh^2(psi) pi_psi^2. This should be re-derived.
- Potential-sign issue: the Hamiltonian includes + mu^2 cos(3 theta_4). Around theta_4 = 0, this has negative quadratic curvature if read literally, yet the document reports omega_theta^2 = k^2 + 3 mu^2. The potential sign, expansion point, and normalization need review.
- The tau sector is appended only as optional H_tau, not dynamically derived.
- No full Dirac-Bergmann constraint analysis or first-class proof is shown.
- The 3+1D lift is asserted to be straightforward but not demonstrated.
