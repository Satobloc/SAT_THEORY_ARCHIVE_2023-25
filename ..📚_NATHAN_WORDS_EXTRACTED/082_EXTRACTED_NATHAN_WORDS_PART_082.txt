The critique regarding the **Parameter Calibration** challenges the core methodology of the theory, suggesting a point that cannot be solved without redefining the theory's foundational premise.

*   **Why it Appears Insoluble:** A theory claiming to be based entirely on geometry must **derive** $\alpha$ (or $R$) from internal geometric constants without recourse to an empirical input. The provided sources explicitly state that $R$ is *structurally fixed* by anchoring the mass scale. To "solve" this critique, the author must calculate the value of $m_{\rm eff}(Q=1)$ and the ratio $R$ *purely* from the geometric constants ($T, \ell_f, \kappa$, etc. in the Scaling Relations), thereby eliminating the need for the empirical input. As the text currently defines the anchoring process as the mechanism by which $R$ is fixed, this mathematical dependency seems foundational to the current structure, rendering its "derivation" (as opposed to its calculation following input) problematic.
