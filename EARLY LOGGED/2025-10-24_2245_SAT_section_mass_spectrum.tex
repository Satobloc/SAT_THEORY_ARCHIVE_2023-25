\section{Mass Spectrum Prediction and Current Limitations}
\label{sec:mass_spectrum}

\subsection{Simplified SAT Mass Formula}

The working ansatz for composite bundle masses is
\[
m(Q) \;=\; \frac{m_0}{Q} \;+\; \beta\,w \;+\; \delta_f ,
\]
with $m_0 = 0.30~\text{GeV}$ (tension scale),
$\beta = 0.14~\text{GeV}$ (vibrational increment),
winding number $w$, and flavour correction $\delta_f$ (0 for $u/d$, $0.1$--$0.2$
for $s$, and $1.2$ for $c$).

\subsection{Interpretation}

\paragraph{Successes.}  The $1/Q$ scaling plus a single vibrational parameter
captures the qualitative ordering of hadron masses and reproduces heavy--flavour
states at the $\mathcal{O}(40\%)$ level with only three global parameters.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Pion anomaly} ($\pi$ mass overestimated by $>100\%$):
          indicates missing chiral--symmetry protection.
    \item \textbf{Lepton sector} ($\mu$ mass error $\sim300\%$):
          suggests that leptons are not governed by the same
          topological mass formula as hadrons.
    \item \textbf{Baryon underprediction} ($p,n$ underestimated by $>70\%$):
          points to an additional Borromean binding energy not included here.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Planned Corrections.}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \emph{Chiral term} $\gamma_\chi$ for pseudo--Goldstone bundles to
          lower the $\pi$ mass specifically.
    \item Separate \emph{lepton mass module} (SAT.O10) treating $Q=0/1$
          non--topological bundles with defect energy scaling.
    \item \emph{Q--dependent tension} $m_0(Q) = a + b\log Q$ to raise
          $Q=3$ baryon masses without disrupting meson fits.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Falsifiability.}
Significant future deviations (factor $>2$) from any revised SAT spectrum will
constitute a direct falsification of the mass--generation mechanism proposed
in Modules~SAT.O9--O10.
