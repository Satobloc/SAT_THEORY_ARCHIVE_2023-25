Glossary of Symbols and Terms

θ4(x): Scalar field representing the local angular deviation between filament tangent vector v^μ and the emergent time-flow vector u^μ(x). Diagnostic only; not a source of mass.
u^μ(x): Emergent time-flow vector field derived from local filament current density. Normalized as u^μ u_μ = -1.
S_{μν}(x): Strain tensor encoding local bundle distortion, defined as: S_{μν} = ∇_μ u_ν + ∇_ν u_μ.
S(x): Strain scalar, defined as S(x) = sqrt(S_{μν}(x) S^{μν}(x)). Quantifies local misalignment of the time vector field and governs emergent proper time rate and entropy production.
ρ_wind(x), ρ_link(x), ρ_triplet(x): Topological density fields counting filament winding, linking, and triple-linking respectively. Used to define gauge coupling constants.
g_G(x): Emergent gauge coupling constant, defined via g_G^{-2}(x) ∼ ρ_G(x) ℓ_f^2, where G ∈ {U(1), SU(2), SU(3)}.
Q: Topological suppression index combining winding, linking, and writhing of filament bundles. Appears in m_eff = m_0 / Q.
φ(x): Foliation scalar field labeling hypersurfaces of constant emergent time. Gradient yields u^μ.
dτ(x): Emergent proper time increment, defined locally as dτ = S(x) dφ.
