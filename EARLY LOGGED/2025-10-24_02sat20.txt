
SAT 20 — Glossary and Model Index (Version 1)

Symbol / Term               | Definition
-----------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------
M                            | 4-dimensional differentiable manifold
γ : ℝ → M                    | Worldline — smooth curve parameterized by λ
v^μ                          | Tangent vector to the worldline; v^μ = dx^μ/dλ
[∇]                          | Projective Structure — equivalence class of affine connections defining unparameterized geodesics
Ω                            | Nowhere-vanishing 4-form — Volume Form on M
τ                            | Nowhere-vanishing 1-form — Time Volume Element on M
m                            | Mass — scalar invariant defined as m = τ_μ v^μ
S                            | Action: S = m ∫ τ (reparameterization-invariant action for free massive particle)
Equation of Motion           | v^ν ∂_ν τ_μ = 0 — conservation of time volume element along the worldline

Notes:
- All structures defined with minimal assumptions.
- No metric, connection, or gauge fields inserted a priori.
- No hidden tunable parameters allowed.
- This version captures Stage 0 (Manifold and Worldline) through Stage 2 (Mass Introduction).
