\subsection{WP0.3 Consistency Proof (sketch)}
Define the ensemble co–metric
\[
\tilde g_{\mu\nu}(x)=\langle v_{\mu}v_{\nu}\rangle_{F(x)},\qquad
v_{\mu}=\dot\gamma_{\mu}.
\]

Therefore
\[
g_{\mu\nu}=(\tilde g)^{-1}_{\mu\nu},

yielding a Lorentzian metric sourced solely by filament statistics.
