\section*{Overview}
This module defines the emergent mechanism by which mass is dynamically suppressed, modulated, and quantized within the SAT.4D formalism. Mass arises from filament geometry, is suppressed by topological class \( Q \), and modulated by curvature and entropy-based strain.

\section*{1. Geometric Mass Scale}
From filament tension \( T \) and rigidity \( A \), define:
\[
\ell_f = \left( \frac{2A}{T} \right)^{1/3}, \quad m_0 = \frac{T}{\ell_f}
\]
This sets the intrinsic vibrational scale of a free SAT filament.

\section*{2. Topological Mass Suppression}
Topological class \( Q \in \mathbb{Z}_+ \) (e.g., from O8) defines a suppression factor:
\[
m_{\text{eff}} = \frac{m_0}{Q}
\]

\section*{3. Phase-Based Class Locking}
From SAT bundle phase quantization (D7):
\[
\Delta \phi = \phi_1 - \phi_2 = \frac{2\pi k}{n}, \quad Q \propto n
\]
This encodes discrete allowed states based on filament phase locking:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \( n=1 \): Neutrino-like states
  \item \( n=2 \): Spinor bundles (Dirac fermions)
  \item \( n=3 \): SU(3) triplet (baryons)
\end{itemize}

\section*{4. Geometry-Curvature Modulation}
Allow \( Q \) to evolve with shear scalar \( S(\phi) \), e.g.:
\[
Q(\phi) = Q_0 + \gamma S(\phi), \quad S(\phi) = S_0 + \beta \phi
\]
Curvature (from O2/O6) contributes:
\[
m_{\text{eff}}(\phi) = \frac{m_0}{Q(\phi)} + \alpha R(\phi)
\]

\section*{5. Final Lagrangian Form}
The quantum mass-coupled SAT Dirac Lagrangian is:
\[
\mathcal{L}_{\text{QMC}} =
\bar\Psi \left(i\gamma^\mu \nabla_\mu - \left[\frac{m_0}{Q(\phi)} + \alpha R(\phi)\right]\right) \Psi
\]
