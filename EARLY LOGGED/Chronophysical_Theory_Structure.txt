
Chronophysical Structure Proposition: Core Framework

I. Foundational Concepts
   A. Filaments
      - Represent fermionic matter
      - Oriented structures in four-dimensional spacetime
   B. Time Surface
      - Propagating boundary (not a substance) that energizes filament cross-sections
      - Reveals structure by imparting energy, not merely 'slicing' space
   C. Aligned Filaments
      - Parallel to the time surface
      - Do not resist sweep, define 'space'
   D. Misaligned Filaments
      - Resist the sweep
      - Define 'matter' through structural coherence across time

II. Structural Logic
   A. Energy Input from Time Surface
      - Energizes filament structures based on angle of incidence
      - Imparts varying energy loads depending on geometry
   B. Absorption Outcomes
      - Below threshold: dormancy
      - At threshold: structure resolution (stable matter)
      - Above threshold: radiative overflow (light)
   C. Radiative Overflow Principle
      - Excess energy escapes via aligned filaments as light (bosonic ripples)
      - Stars and energetic events are pressure regulators

III. Dynamics of Interaction
   A. Light
      - Not a product of matter alone but of structural overflow into aligned space
      - Requires matter-space interface to manifest
   B. Cross-Temporal Tension
      - Filaments under tension may exert influence on past states
      - Observable in high-energy astrophysical phenomena (e.g., pre-GRB flashes)
   C. Collapse Regimes
      - Extreme overstrain leads to decoherence, breakdown, or exotic emission (jets, gamma bursts)

IV. Coherence with Observations
   A. QED and Photon Emission
      - Photon emergence reframed as energy redirection at constraint boundaries
   B. Relativity and c
      - Light speed preserved through structural constancy of aligned filaments
   C. Cosmological Light
      - CMB interpreted as early-universe structural overflow
      - Redshift from evolving filament-time surface angles

V. Architectural Coherence
   - Space = coherent alignment
   - Matter = constrained misalignment
   - Light = overflow transmission through space
   - Time = energetic sweep, not emergent sequence
