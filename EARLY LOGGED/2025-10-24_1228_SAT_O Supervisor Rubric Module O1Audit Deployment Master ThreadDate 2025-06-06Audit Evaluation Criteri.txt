Audit Deployment: Master Thread
Date: 2025-06-06

Audit Evaluation Criteria
------------------------------------------------------------
# | Audit Requirement                     | Pass Criteria                                           | Fail Criteria
--|---------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------
1 | Full Derivations                      | All results explicitly derived.                          | Missing or incomplete derivations.
2 | Dimensional Analysis                  | Units confirmed for all key equations.                   | Missing or incorrect units.
3 | Tensor/Index Verification             | Correct contractions and symmetries.                     | Errors or omissions in tensor work.
4 | Quantization Structure                | Canonical commutators shown.                             | Missing or incorrect quantization.
5 | Falsifiability Pathways               | At least one clear falsifiability test provided.          | No clear falsifiability pathway.
6 | Worked Example                        | Nontrivial example of metric emergence provided.          | No example or trivial example.
7 | Stress Testing                        | Parameter variations explored.                           | Only ideal cases discussed.
8 | Failure Modes Identified              | At least one plausible failure mode discussed.            | No failure modes proposed.
9 | Edge Cases Explored                   | Limits and boundary conditions tested.                   | Ignored extreme limits.
10| Emergent Constant Scrutiny            | Scaling and limit behavior analyzed.                     | Constants assumed without analysis.
11| No Unjustified Passes                  | Every verification justified explicitly.                 | Box-checking without proof.
12| Mathematical and Physical Consistency | No contradictions found.                                 | Contradictions or inconsistencies.
13| Group/Topology Considerations         | Emergent gauge structures analyzed.                      | Topological structures ignored.
14| Quantum Corrections Plausibility      | Quantum effects discussed plausibly.                     | Quantum effects omitted.
15| Explicit Calculations for Emergent Structures | Emergent fields and Planck scale derived.         | Only actions stated without derivation.
16| No Improvement                        | No "fixes" or suggestions proposed.                       | Theory improvements attempted.
17| Boundary Conditions Check             | Normalizability and stability verified.                   | Boundary conditions omitted.
18| Mathematical Completeness             | No missing steps or gaps.                                 | Derivation gaps found.
19| Critical Commentary                   | Balanced evaluation of strengths and weaknesses.          | Only praise or vague comments.
20| Surprising/Nontrivial Tests            | Performed at least one nontrivial test beyond checklist.   | Only checklist items addressed.

Scoring System
------------------------------------------------------------
Score   | Verdict
--------|----------------------------------------------------
18–20   | Ultra-Hostile Pass
15–17   | Conditional Pass (second review needed)
<15     | Fail (audit redo required)
