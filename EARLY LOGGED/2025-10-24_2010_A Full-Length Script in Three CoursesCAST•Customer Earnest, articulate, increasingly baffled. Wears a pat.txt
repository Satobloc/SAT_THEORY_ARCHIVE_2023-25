A Full-Length Script in Three Courses
CAST:
	•	Customer: Earnest, articulate, increasingly baffled. Wears a patch that reads “Culinary Folklorist.”
	•	Waiter: Cheerfully combative. Insists he’s “just doing his job” while rewriting the rules every 4.5 minutes.
	•	AutoDing™: An always-hovering, intermittently zapping cylinder with one glowing eye, one meat-sensing whisker, and a paddle labeled “SHAME.”
	•	BubbleTender: A mute, gliding figure who dispenses colorful, morally ambiguous beverages and silently judges.
	•	Manager (eventually): A holographic fax machine in a meat-locker.

ACT I: The Ordering Ritual
Customer (sitting, enthusiastic): All right! Been waiting all week. I’ll have the kangaroo burger—medium rare, side of sunchoke crisps.
AutoDing™ (spins up, eye glows red): “Caution: Use of ‘rare’ implies scarcity economics. Do: practice egalitarian distribution. Don’t: medium.” [zap.!]
Customer: Ouch!
Waiter (arriving, offended): Sir. I heard that.
Customer (blinking): What, the... word "medium"?
Waiter: We do not use capitalist measurements here. Please use the menu’s designated doneness scale.
Customer (flipping menu): What is “Tarot-Advised Warmth Level 4b”?
Waiter (already sipping NijiBubble): Slightly penitent. Texturally contrite. It may whisper during chewing.
Customer (hesitant): Okay. Then one kangaroo burger. Whispery. No sides.
Waiter (horrified): You can’t order the kangaroo burger. It’s marked “Available for a Limited Time Only.”
Customer: Right. Which means it’s available… for a limited time.
Waiter (scoffing): No, no. It means it’s not available. That’s the limitation. That’s the time. It’s over.
Customer (incredulous): Then why’s it on the menu?
Waiter (gesturing to wall): For reference. Like the Dead Sea Scrolls.

ACT II: Attempts at Compliance
Customer (sighing, flipping through menu again): Fine. I’ll have the Monte Cristo—but just the toast. No ham, no cheese. No meat. No implications of meat.
AutoDing™: “Error: Toast is a euphemism for ‘sinner’s crisp.’ Do: confess. Don’t: sear.” [zap.!]
Customer: Ouch!
Waiter (writing something on a napkin): That’s your fifth infraction. Attempted substitution.
Customer: What?! I removed the meat!
Waiter: Which implies it was once there. That’s aggressive nostalgia.
BubbleTender (glides by, offers a glowing pink NijiBubble with a winking schoolgirl floating inside)
Customer (frowning): And what’s this?
Waiter (dreamily): That’s the !!!ゴゴ❤ゴックン❤️ベイビー❤ガル!!!. It contains four gas bubbles, fermented meringue, and the concept of consent, loosely interpreted.
Customer (narrowing eyes): Is it meat?
Waiter (offended): Sir, it’s Niji. It’s color-coded to bypass the meat spectrum entirely. Any similarities to ortolan, eel, or pubescent sorrow are strictly aesthetic.

ACT III: Attempts at Reason
Customer: Listen. I don’t want to be difficult. I just want to understand. Are there any menu items that are meatless and won’t get me zapped?
Waiter (pauses): Well... there's the Symbolic Lentil.
Customer (relieved): Great. I’ll take that.
Waiter: Unfortunately, it’s conceptual. You’ll need to imagine it, and then apologize.
Customer (deadpan): Is there anything I can physically ingest?
AutoDing™: “Caution: Ingestion implies ownership. Do: chew abstractly. Don’t: embody.” [zap.!]
Customer: Ouch!

ACT IV: Collapse of Consent
Customer (desperate): Fine! Tap water! Just give me tap water!
Waiter (waving toward bar): BubbleTender?
BubbleTender glides back with a cup of water shimmering with iridescent oil. A pork rind floats on top like a smug canoe.
Customer (whispering): Is that meat?
Waiter (chewing on one): No. That’s mouth-skin. Totally legal.
AutoDing™ (charging): “Violation: Use of whispered inquiry. Do: proclaim. Don’t: cast doubt.” [zap.!]
Customer: Ouch!
Customer (finally snapping): AutoDing, I want nothing. Just air. Unscented. Uncooked. Unserved.
AutoDing™: “Serving: Velvety Air reduction with garnish of boar essence, unspoken.” [zap.!]
Customer: Ouch!

EPILOGUE: The Exit
Manager (fax machine lowers from ceiling, spooling endlessly): Dear valued diner. You are now banned for continued menu misconduct. Refunds are not available. But thank you for participating in our evolving dining experience. Your record will be shared with The Next Restaurant.
Waiter (beaming): And that, sir, is the beauty of a steakhouse with no meat.
Customer (sitting back in disbelief): I’m going to Yelp about this.
AutoDing™ (menacingly): “Do: scream into apricot. Don’t: Yelp.” [zap.!]
Customer: Ouch!

[CURTAIN FALLS]
