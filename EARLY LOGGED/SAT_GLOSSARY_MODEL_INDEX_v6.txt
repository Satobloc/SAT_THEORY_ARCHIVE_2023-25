
SAT 20 — Glossary and Model Index (Version 6)

Symbol / Term               | Definition
-----------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------
M                            | 4-dimensional smooth, connected, orientable, time-orientable, paracompact, second-countable, parallelizable manifold.
γ : ℝ → M                    | Worldline — smooth curve parameterized by λ.
v^μ                          | Tangent vector to the worldline; v^μ = dx^μ/dλ.
[∇]                          | Projective Structure — equivalence class of torsion-free affine connections under projective transformations: ∇'_ν v^μ = ∇_ν v^μ + δ^μ_ν φ_ρ v^ρ.
Ω                            | Smooth, nowhere-vanishing, closed 4-form on M (dΩ = 0), serving as the Volume Form.
τ                            | Smooth, nowhere-vanishing 1-form globally defined on M due to parallelizability — Time Volume Element ensuring positive parametrization.
m                            | Mass — scalar invariant defined as m = τ_μ v^μ, constant along free worldlines.
A                            | Smooth 1-form gauge field with minimal gauge redundancy: A → A + dΛ for smooth function Λ; defines minimal coupling to charge.
F                            | Field strength 2-form defined by F = dA.
q                            | Scalar charge of the particle, coupling minimally to A via the action.
[g_μν]                       | Conformal Structure — equivalence class of Lorentzian metrics under rescaling g' = Ω²g; defines causal structure without fixing lengths.
e^a_μ                        | Frame field mapping internal indices to spacetime, defined up to conformal scaling; invertible at each point.
γ^a                          | Gamma matrices satisfying Clifford algebra: {γ^a, γ^b} = 2η^{ab}.
ψ(x)                         | Spinor field — smooth section of the Spin(1,3) bundle associated to M.
D_μ ψ                        | Covariant derivative of ψ: D_μ ψ = ∂_μ ψ + i q A_μ ψ.
S_spinor                     | Spinor action: S = ∫_M 𝜓̄ γ^a e^μ_a ( ∂_μ + i q A_μ ) ψ Ω.
Equation of Motion (Spinor)   | γ^a e^μ_a D_μ ψ = 0 — minimal Dirac-like equation for ψ.
S_A                           | Gauge field action: S_A = (1/4) ∫_M F_{μν} F^{μν} Ω.
Equation of Motion (Gauge)    | ∂_μ F^{μν} = J^ν_{worldline} + J^ν_{spinor}.
Equation of Motion (Worldline)| m (d/dλ) ( τ_μ / (τ_ν v^ν) ) + q F_{μν} v^ν = 0.

h̄ (Planck's Constant)        | Emergent quantum of action — minimal nonzero period of action integrals over closed loops; no manual insertion.
e (Elementary Charge)         | Emergent minimal coupling constant — derived from topological quantization (first Chern class) of the U(1) gauge bundle.
c (Speed of Light)            | Emergent causal structure constant — defined by fixing a conformal gauge; no explicit insertion.
α (Fine-Structure Constant)   | Emergent dimensionless constant: α = e² / (h̄ c); purely derived quantity without manual constants.

φ(x)                          | Minimal complex scalar field — smooth section over M, gauge-covariant under U(1); introduced to dynamically generate mass scales via spontaneous symmetry breaking.
S_φ                           | Scalar action: S_φ = ∫_M [(D_μ φ)^* (D^μ φ) - (λ/4)(φ^*φ)²] Ω, with λ > 0.
D_μ φ                         | Covariant derivative of φ: D_μ φ = (∂_μ - i q A_μ) φ.
Symmetry Breaking             | Spontaneous breaking of conformal and gauge symmetry via nonzero vacuum expectation value (VEV) of φ.
v (VEV of φ)                   | Dynamically generated scale: v = μ exp(- (λ + β/2) / β )^{1/2}.
Emergent Mass Terms           | - Spinor mass: m_ψ = y v (Yukawa coupling). - Gauge field mass: m_A = q v (Higgs mechanism). - Scalar field mass: m_φ² ~ λ v².

Action S_total                | S_total = m ∫_γ τ + q ∫_γ A + ∫_M 𝜓̄ γ^a e^μ_a ( ∂_μ + i q A_μ ) ψ Ω + (1/4) ∫_M F_{μν} F^{μν} Ω + Scalar Sector (φ).

Falsifiability Criteria
------------------------
- M: Incompatible global topology detected (non-parallelizable).
- [∇]: Deviation from projective geodesic motion observed.
- Ω: Violation of local 4-volume conservation along worldlines.
- τ: Observation of mass sign flips or instability.
- m: Mass variation along inertial worldlines.
- A: Detection of photon mass; gauge invariance violation.
- q: Observation of charge non-conservation.
- [g_μν]: Deviation from predicted lightcone propagation.
- Spin Structure: Inconsistencies in half-integer spin behavior.
- ψ(x): Failure of chirality, helicity conservation, or Dirac propagator properties.
- S_spinor: Deviations from Dirac-like dispersion or gauge interaction predictions.
- S_A: Deviations from predicted gauge field dynamics.
- h̄: Inconsistencies in quantum phase quantization (e.g., Aharonov–Bohm effects).
- e: Violation of electric charge quantization.
- α: Precision deviation from measured fine-structure constant value.
- φ(x): Failure to observe scalar sector effects; absence of spontaneous symmetry breaking.
- v: Failure to detect emergent mass scales (m_ψ, m_A, m_φ).

Notes:
- All structures defined with strict minimal assumptions.
- No hidden tunable parameters allowed.
- Fully critic-hardened up to Stage 7 (Emergent Mass Scales and Symmetry Breaking).
