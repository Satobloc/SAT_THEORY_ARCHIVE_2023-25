\section{Topological Prediction of Gauge Coupling Ratios}
\label{sec:gauge_ratios}

\subsection{SAT Basis: Mode Density and Coupling Strengths}

From Module~\textbf{SAT.O3}, each gauge coupling arises from a distinct
topological mode density:
\[
g_1 \sim \rho_{\wind},
\qquad
g_2 \sim \rho_{\link},
\qquad
g_3 \sim \rho_{\braid}.
\]
The relative strengths are set by the frequency of these modes within
the SAT filament network, independently of any Lagrangian field strength
normalizations.

A lattice enumeration in Phase~II produced the normalized densities:
\[
\rho_{\wind} : \rho_{\link} : \rho_{\braid}
\;\simeq\;
0.15 : 0.27 : 0.41,
\]
yielding the SAT-predicted couplings:
\[
g_1^{\SAT} = 0.15, \quad
g_2^{\SAT} = 0.27, \quad
g_3^{\SAT} = 0.41.
\]

\subsection{Ratio Comparison with Experiment}

To eliminate scale ambiguities, we compare only \emph{ratios} of couplings
with those extracted from experiment at the electroweak scale $M_Z$.
PDG 2025 values are:
\[
g_1^{\PDG} = 0.357, \quad
g_2^{\PDG} = 0.652, \quad
g_3^{\PDG} = 1.221.
\]

\subsection{Interpretation and Falsifiability}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Correct hierarchy.} SAT reproduces the qualitative ordering
$g_1 < g_2 < g_3$, which is not enforced a priori in the topological counts.
\item \textbf{Quantitative match.} Ratios agree with PDG values to within
$\sim 20\%$ without renormalization-group running or model-dependent
normalizations.
\item \textbf{Improvement pathway.} Module~\textbf{SAT.O3.6} proposes a
scale--dependent mode-density renormalization $\rho_i(\mu)$ that may close
the remaining gap.
\item \textbf{Falsifiability.} A reversal of the ordering, or exact ratios
outside 30\% tolerance, would challenge the topological origin of couplings.
\end{itemize}
