
SAT 20 — Glossary and Model Index (Version 2)

Symbol / Term               | Definition
-----------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------
M                            | 4-dimensional smooth, connected, orientable, time-orientable, paracompact, second-countable, parallelizable manifold.
γ : ℝ → M                    | Worldline — smooth curve parameterized by λ.
v^μ                          | Tangent vector to the worldline; v^μ = dx^μ/dλ.
[∇]                          | Projective Structure — equivalence class of torsion-free affine connections under projective transformations: ∇'_ν v^μ = ∇_ν v^μ + δ^μ_ν φ_ρ v^ρ.
Ω                            | Smooth, nowhere-vanishing, closed 4-form on M (dΩ = 0), serving as the Volume Form.
τ                            | Smooth, nowhere-vanishing 1-form globally defined on M due to parallelizability — Time Volume Element ensuring positive parametrization.
m                            | Mass — scalar invariant defined as m = τ_μ v^μ, constant along free worldlines.
A                            | Smooth 1-form gauge field with minimal gauge redundancy: A → A + dΛ for smooth function Λ; defines minimal coupling to charge.
F                            | Field strength 2-form defined by F = dA.
q                            | Scalar charge of the particle, coupling minimally to A via the action.

Action S                     | S = m ∫_γ τ + q ∫_γ A — reparameterization-invariant action for free massive charged particle.

Equation of Motion (τ)        | v^ν ∂_ν τ_μ = 0 — conservation of the time volume element along the worldline.
Gauge Invariance (A)          | Invariance under A → A + dΛ; ensures masslessness and conservation laws.
Field Strength (F)            | F = dA ensures propagation of the gauge field.

Falsifiability Criteria
------------------------
- M: Incompatible global topology detected (non-parallelizable).
- [∇]: Deviation from projective geodesic motion observed.
- Ω: Violation of local 4-volume conservation along worldlines.
- τ: Observation of mass sign flips or instability.
- m: Mass variation along inertial worldlines.
- A: Detection of photon mass; gauge invariance violation.
- q: Observation of charge non-conservation.

Notes:
- All structures defined with strict minimal assumptions.
- No metric, no pre-assumed connection beyond projective class.
- No hidden tunable parameters allowed.
- Fully critic-hardened up to Stage 3 (Mass and Charge Introduction).
