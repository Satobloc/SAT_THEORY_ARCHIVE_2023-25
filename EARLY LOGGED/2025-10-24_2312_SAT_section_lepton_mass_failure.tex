\section{Lepton Mass Hierarchy: Failure of the Pure Topology Model}
\label{sec:lepton_mass_failure}

\subsection{Minimal Q = 1 Ansatz}

For single–filament bundles ($Q=1$) we postulate a two–term energy model
\[
m_\ell \;=\; \beta\,w \;+\; \gamma\,\tau ,
\]
with
\begin{itemize}
  \item $w$ : internal winding number (vibrational energy),
  \item $\tau$ : external torsion level (filament twist),
  \item $(\beta,\gamma)$ : universal energy scales to be fitted.
\end{itemize}
No flavour–specific defect tension ($\delta_f$) is included at this stage.

\end{center}

\subsection{Least–Squares Fit}

Fitting $(\beta,\gamma)$ to the PDG masses
$m_e=0.511~\text{MeV},\; m_\mu=105.7~\text{MeV},\;
m_\tau=1777~\text{MeV}$ yields
\[
\beta = 7.33\times 10^{-14}\,\text{MeV}, \qquad
\gamma = 7.32\times 10^{2}\,\text{MeV}.
\]

\subsection{Interpretation}

\paragraph{Success.}
The model reproduces the \emph{ordering}
$m_e < m_\mu < m_\tau$,
confirming that higher winding/torsion maps to heavier leptons.

\paragraph{Failure.}
Quantitatively the model breaks down:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Electron mass is \emph{essentially zero} in this framework.
  \item Muon mass overshoots by nearly an order of magnitude.
  \item Only the tau comes within $20\%$.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Diagnostic Implications}

Pure topology ($w,\tau$) is \emph{insufficient}.  A third term is required:
\[
m_\ell = \beta w + \gamma \tau + \delta_f,
\]
where $\delta_f$ encodes flavour–specific defect tension.
Physically, this may arise from filament shape discontinuities or coupling
to vacuum strain not captured by $w,\tau$.

\paragraph{Next module.}
SAT.O10 will introduce $\delta_f$ and refit the triplet
$(\beta,\gamma,\delta_f)$, aiming to reproduce
$m_e,m_\mu,m_\tau$ within $<10\%$.

\paragraph{Falsifiability.}
If no combination of $(w,\tau,\delta_f)$ can match the lepton spectrum
without \emph{ad hoc} flavour parameters, SAT’s claim of geometric mass
generation for leptons fails.
