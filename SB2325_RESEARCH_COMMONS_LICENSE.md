## 1. Purpose

SB2325 is maintained as an open research, development, and provenance archive. The purpose of these terms is to keep research, scholarship, criticism, teaching, testing, and independent investigation broadly open while preserving attribution and a fair relationship between the archive, substantial commercial use of protected archive material, the originator, and—at exceptional scale—the wider commons.

The archive holder does not object to commerce, profit, employment, or private enterprise merely because they involve ideas encountered here. The concern is narrower: material private economic value should not be systematically extracted from protected SB2325 material while its identifiable source contribution is erased and its originator excluded from the resulting value.

Nothing in these terms claims ownership of facts, mathematical truths, laws of nature, independently discovered ideas, or other subject matter that applicable law does not permit the archive holder to control.
