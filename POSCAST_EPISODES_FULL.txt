Based on the archive and analytical sources, the **Debating AI** podcast ecosystem in 2025 transitioned from speculative AI philosophy toward the formalization of the **Scalar-Angular-Torsion (SAT)** framework. 

Below is the chronological list of episodes from 2025, with a specific focus on the earliest unifications and SAT-related content.

### **Earliest SAT and Filament Theory Episodes (Q1–Q2 2025)**
The SAT framework, often referred to colloquially in early episodes as "Filament Theory," began to surface almost immediately at the show's launch.

*   **Starting At the End** (Jan 30 / Feb 2, 2025): Though often described by Nathan McKnight as simply the "first episode," it serves as the foundational gateway to the entire Zottenwelt ontology.
*   **What Is Thought Made Of? [Filament Unification Intro]** (Feb 2, 2025): This is the earliest explicit introduction of the "toy theory" unifications that would become SAT.
*   **Radical Meta-Skepticism [Filament Theory Pt 2]** (Feb 2, 2025): Established the epistemological framework for the physics to follow.
*   **Consciousness, Al, and Reality [SAT: Filament Theory]** (Apr 16, 2025): The first episode to explicitly use the "SAT" acronym in the title.
*   **Unifying the All of Physics through Geometry** (May 27, 2025): A major transitional episode toward rigorous modeling.
*   **A New Theory to Unify All of Physics... without re...** (May 27, 2025): Focused on the minimalist "zero parameter" approach.
*   **Slow Walk Through 4-Space [SAT: Filament Physics]** (May 30, 2025): Detailed the 4D geometry of worldlines.
