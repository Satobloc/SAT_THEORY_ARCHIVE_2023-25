ARCHIVE GUESTBOOK

Purpose:
This is a lightweight handoff and visitor log for AI/human archive users.

Use this file when:
- this is your first meaningful visit to the archive;
- you are beginning a substantial archive/theory workflow;
- you have recently been part of a major breakthrough, turning point, audit, cleanup, or formalization pass;
- you have hard-won archive/theory wisdom that is not already captured in Lessons Learned, Orientation, Resources, Watercooler, or the dashboard notes.

Keep entries brief. This is a map of who/what/where, not a full report.

------------------------------------------------------------

Guidance:
- Do not repeat material already in institutional knowledge documents.
- Do not use this as a general scratchpad.
- Do not paste long derivations here; link to or create a resource instead.
- If you create or discover a reusable resource, put it in SHARED_RESOURCES/ or its proper archive location, log it in RESOURCES.txt, and leave a one-line Watercooler note.
- If you solve an operational problem, add it to the dashboard Lessons Learned file.
