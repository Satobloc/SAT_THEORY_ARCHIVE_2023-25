STAGING WAYFINDING

This folder is archive machinery, not SAT primary material.

Use it as a holding area when edits, requests, or resources are too large, fragile, or unfinished for direct placement.

Basic path:
- INBOX: raw incoming material.
- PARTS: chunked pieces waiting to be assembled.
- READY: reviewed material ready for Archive Admin or manual move.
- RUNNING: active staged work.
- DONE: completed staged work or receipts.
- FAILED: failed staged work needing diagnosis.

Principle:
Assemble. Review. Then commit.
