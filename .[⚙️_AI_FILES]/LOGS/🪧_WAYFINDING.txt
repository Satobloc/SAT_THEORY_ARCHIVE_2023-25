🪧 WAYFINDING — LOGS

STATUS: STUB / NEEDS REVIEW

Purpose:
This folder contains workflow, tool, indexing, extraction, and operational logs.

This is evidence, not theory.

Use logs to:
- understand what happened;
- debug workflows;
- reconstruct decisions;
- investigate failures;
- confirm successful operations.

Guidance:
- Do not treat logs as canonical theory sources.
- Significant lessons belong in the dashboard Lessons Learned file.
- Significant reusable resources belong in SHARED_RESOURCES and RESOURCES.txt.
- Error patterns should eventually become documented solutions.

Visitor request:
If you identify recurring failures, bottlenecks, or workflow patterns, consider summarizing them in institutional knowledge documents rather than leaving them buried in logs.
