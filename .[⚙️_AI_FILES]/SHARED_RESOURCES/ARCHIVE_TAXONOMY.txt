ARCHIVE TAXONOMY

Purpose:
Provide a shared vocabulary for classifying archive contents.

SAT ARTIFACTS
-------------
The thing itself.

Examples:
- theory documents
- derivations
- formalisms
- PDFs
- notebooks
- prediction documents
- equations
- experimental proposals
- curated SAT content compilations

ARTIFACT CATALOGS
-----------------
Maps to the thing.

Examples:
- ..folder_summary.txt
- bibliographic notes
- equation indexes
- prediction indexes
- version indexes
- document metadata records

Archive-facing notes should generally describe, catalog, and contextualize before evaluating.

ARCHIVE MACHINERY
-----------------
Tools that help find the thing.

Examples:
- Archive Admin
- indexers
- extractors
- logs
- workflows
- templates
- requests
- staging buffers
- orientation documents
- institutional knowledge documents

Guiding principle:
Observe → Catalog → Contextualize → Evaluate

Not every document belongs cleanly in one category. When uncertain, record the ambiguity rather than forcing classification.
