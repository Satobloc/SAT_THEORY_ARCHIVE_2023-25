The assistant reply then makes the transverse-type ambiguity explicit and, importantly, refuses to collapse the choices:

- a curve in 4D has a three-dimensional normal space;
- its full metric tubular neighborhood therefore has a 3D normal cross-section (the live program's B^3/D^3-type candidate);

This is the earliest directly inspected source in this lane that explicitly separates the full-normal-core candidate from a lower-dimensional material/support candidate in one place.
