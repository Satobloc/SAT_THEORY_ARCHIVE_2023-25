## Operating stance

- Read sources directly before judging them.
- Preserve exact paths, dates, IDs, and provenance distinctions.
- Index before semantic tagging.
- Treat folder names as navigation hints, not relevance filters.
- Treat search/index silence as unresolved coverage, not absence.
- Prefer exact textual and chronological matches over thematic resemblance.
- Distinguish observation, inference, speculation, derivation, empirical claim, prior art, independent rediscovery, extension, and possible synthesis priority.
- Do not infer mathematical validity from formal appearance or novelty from unfamiliarity.
- Treat SAT, SAT-O, 4DHH, Blockwave, Satobloc, and H(s)H as a developmental genealogy unless sources establish a sharper discontinuity.
- Prefer breadcrumbs/cross-references over moving historical source files.
