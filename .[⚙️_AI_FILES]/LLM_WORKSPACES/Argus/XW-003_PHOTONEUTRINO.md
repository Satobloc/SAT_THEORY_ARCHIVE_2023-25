## External comparison boundary

Historical `neutrino theory of light` literature is strong prior art for a deep photon–neutrino structural relationship, but it is not substantively identical to the checked SAT construction. Jordan-era models in the 1930s attempted to build photon/Bose amplitudes from neutrino degrees of freedom; contemporary discussions describe photon emission via neutrino/antineutrino processes or a neutrino Raman-like mechanism. This knocks out any broad novelty formulation such as `photons and neutrinos may have a common deeper construction` or `photon behavior may emerge from neutrino degrees of freedom`.

The narrower SAT compound checked here is different: one single-coil excitation changes observed particle identity by orientation/projection rather than a photon being a neutrino–antineutrino composite. No exact antecedent for that specific single-excitation rotational identity was established in this pass. Classify it as a **possible independent synthesis/extension candidate**, not an established novelty result.

## Bosonic-neutrino branch

The later SAT phrase `neutrinos are true bosons` must be audited separately from photon–neutrino identity. Bosonic or mixed-statistics neutrinos have explicit prior literature; e.g. Dolgov, Hansen, and Smirnov (2005) analyze spin-half neutrinos obeying Bose or mixed statistics. More recent cosmological work (Dai and Liao, arXiv:2501.12264, 2025) reports purely bosonic neutrinos disfavored at 95% confidence in their analysis. Therefore `neutrinos can be bosonic` is not a SAT priority candidate by itself.

What would have to be distinctive is a derived SAT/H(s)H mechanism connecting the proposed orientation/projection map to the observed fermionic versus bosonic state algebra. The source passages inspected here do not supply that bridge.

## Mathematical/physical vetting target

A spatial or higher-dimensional rotation acting within a fixed particle representation does not by itself change spin/statistics class. The SAT construction therefore needs an explicit projection/reduction map that explains, rather than labels, the transition between the neutrino and photon state spaces. A serious vetting pass should require at minimum:

- the domain and codomain of the 4D-to-observed-state map;
- how spin-1/2 neutrino states and spin-1 photon helicity states arise from the proposed common carrier;
- creation/annihilation operator algebra or an equivalent state-counting/statistics construction;
- preservation or emergence of photon gauge structure and transversality;
- compatibility with weak chirality, neutrino flavor mixing/oscillation data, and photon neutrality/mass bounds;
- a dynamical conversion rule and conservation-law accounting, rather than orientation language alone.

Until such a bridge is recovered or constructed, classify the single-excitation identity as `SRC/HISTORICAL-CANDIDATE` and the bosonic-neutrino/statistics consequence as `UNDERDEFINED`.
