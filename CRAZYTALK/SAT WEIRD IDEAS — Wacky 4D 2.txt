a multiply-anchored circulation system with competing global (belt) and local (radial) phase drivers, yielding a tiled field of vortex-like domains whose coherence is repeatedly interrupted and re-seeded at fixed nodal sites

That’s the structural content of the picture, stripped of extra ontology.

If you want to go further, the next sharp question is: what happens when the beltway loop itself becomes phase-dominant over the radial injectors—i.e., when the system crosses from “spoked forcing” to “single dominant circulation mode.”
