[Satobloc/HsH](https://github.com/Satobloc/HsH) and
[SAT_THEORY_ARCHIVE_2023-25](https://github.com/Satobloc/SAT_THEORY_ARCHIVE_2023-25).
It is not a ranking of sources, a declaration of settled policy, or a reason to
ignore material not yet indexed.

## Essential distinction

RMS and SAT are separate projects. RMS is an epistemological and perceptual
framework whose style and constraints influenced SAT's method. It is not an
earlier SAT theory. SAT's own construction history runs from early drawings and
notebooks through successive SAT builds, including SAT-O, 4DHH, Blockwave and
Satobloc, into H(s)H. These are attempts to build and formalize the broad picture
preserved in the Fundamental Intuitions, not rival theories by default.

“Historical” below means earlier in that construction history. It does not mean
discarded. Later visitors may have different questions and authority rules, and
older devices are sometimes productively revived.

## Earliest visual lane

The eight photographed pages in `2003 SKETCHES` and
`SAT_Earliest_Notebook_2003_Documented.pdf` have been visually inspected.
Original page marks visibly include coiled/helical lines crossing a labeled
3-brane, 0/1/2/3-brane diagrams, folded structures, an evolution axis, and an
interaction taxonomy involving collisions, orbits, recombination/bundling,
splitting, and attraction. This supports continuity of SAT's visual
problem-space.

The compilation captions date from 2025 and must not be confused with the 2003
marks. The images do not by themselves prove that later objects, equations, or
interpretations were already fixed.
