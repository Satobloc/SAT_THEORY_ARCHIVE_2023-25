\section*{Why Another ``Time''?}
The core ontology of the theory is a \emph{timeless} four--dimensional block filled with at most three filaments per intersection.  
Nevertheless earlier modules sliced the block by a family of spacelike hypersurfaces $\{\Sigma_t\}$ to \emph{present} observable states and derive Schrödinger evolution (Module~O3-B).  
This raises the worry that a hidden, preferred foliation has been smuggled in.  
Here we show that $\Sigma_t$
\begin{enumerate}
  \item is defined \emph{relationally} from the block geometry plus a coarse--graining scale $\ell_c$, and
  \item evolves by a gauge--fixed flow equation whose integrated form reproduces the unitary propagator of Module~O3-B without introducing new degrees of freedom.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{O3-D.1 \quad Relational Definition of $\Sigma_t$}

Let $N^\mu(x)$ be the unit normal to a compact spacelike hypersurface $\Sigma\subset\mathbb R^{4}$.  
Define its \emph{filament--weighted mean extrinsic curvature}
\[
  \mathcal K[\Sigma] \;=\;
  \frac{1}{\operatorname{Area}(\Sigma)}\,
  \int_{\Sigma}\! d^{3}y\;
  \rho_F(y)\,K(y),
\]
where $K := N^\mu_{;\mu}$ and
$\rho_F(y) := \sum_i \int_0^1 d\sigma\,
  \delta^{4}\!\bigl(y-\gamma_i(\sigma)\bigr)$
is the local filament density.
We impose the relational condition
\[
  \mathcal K[\Sigma_t] \;=\; \frac{t}{\ell_c^{2}}, \qquad t\in\mathbb R,
\tag{D.1}
\]
so that ``time'' is the \emph{running average curvature} measured in units set by the coarse--graining length $\ell_c$.

\paragraph{Remarks.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item If $\ell_c\to\infty$ (no coarse--graining) every hypersurface satisfies \eqref{D.1}, recovering full block invariance.
  \item Different observers with distinct $\ell_c$ obtain \emph{refoliations} related by the gauge symmetry discussed below.
\end{itemize}

\section*{O3-D.2 \quad Gauge--Fixed Flow Equation}

Varying $\Sigma_t$ by a normal deformation $\delta x^\mu = \xi N^\mu$ and demanding $d\mathcal K/dt = 1/\ell_c^{2}$ yields
\[
  \frac{\partial x^\mu}{\partial t}
  \;=\; \xi(x)\,N^\mu(x),
  \quad\text{with}\quad
  \bigl(\Delta_\Sigma -\tfrac{1}{3}R_\Sigma\bigr)\xi
  \;=\; -\,1,
\tag{D.2}
\]
where $\Delta_\Sigma$ and $R_\Sigma$ are the Laplacian and scalar curvature intrinsic to $\Sigma_t$.  Equation~\eqref{D.2} is a \emph{gauge choice} akin to constant--mean--curvature slicing in GR; any other choice connected by a diffeomorphism yields an equivalent description.

\section*{O3-D.3 \quad Compatibility with Block Invariance}

Let $G$ be the group generated by smooth deformations of filaments and embeddings of hypersurfaces.  
Define the BRST charge $Q$ acting on the extended state space
\[
  Q \,\bigl|\Phi,\,\Sigma\bigr\rangle
  = \mathcal L_\xi \bigl|\Phi,\,\Sigma\bigr\rangle,
\]
with $\mathcal L_\xi$ the Lie derivative along $\xi N^\mu$.  
The gauge--fixed propagator is
\[
  U(t_2,t_1) \;=\;
  P\,\exp\!\bigl[-\,i\!\int_{t_1}^{t_2}\! dt\,H_{\text{phys}}\bigr],
  \quad
  H_{\text{phys}} := \{Q,\Psi_{\text{GF}}\},
\tag{D.3}
\]
where $\Psi_{\text{GF}}$ is the gauge--fixing fermion implementing \eqref{D.2}.  
Because $Q^{2}=0$ and $\{Q,\Psi_{\text{GF}}\}$ is $Q$--exact, physical matrix elements (BRST cohomology) are independent of the particular slicing---exactly matching the path--integral result of Module~O3-B.

\section*{O3-D.4 \quad Physical Picture}

Operationally, $\Sigma_t$ tracks the front on which \emph{local decoherence} saturates: once coarse--grained regions of linear size $\ell_c$ accumulate enough filament crossings, their reduced density matrices approach maximal entropy and behave classically.  
Equation~\eqref{D.1} is thus an emergent ``pointer--basis'' condition, while \eqref{D.2} dictates how that front advances into lower--entropy regions without privileging any absolute time.

\section*{Key Takeaways}
\begin{itemize}
  \item $\Sigma_t$ is fixed by a curvature--density condition \emph{internal} to the block; no external clock is referenced.
  \item The flow equation \eqref{D.2} is a gauge choice whose BRST‐exact Hamiltonian leaves physical amplitudes slice‐independent.
  \item Coarse--graining scale $\ell_c$ controls how quantum blocks look classical, linking the slicing to observable decoherence.
\end{itemize}
