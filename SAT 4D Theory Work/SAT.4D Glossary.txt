\section*{Purpose}

This glossary defines the core symbols and terms used throughout SAT.4D. Each entry is grounded in structural definitions, with no assumptions of pre-existing field structures or hidden dynamics. All terms are consistent with the OFFICIAL DIRECTIVES and Mode 1 formalism.

\section*{Glossary of Symbols and Terms}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{\( M \)}: A 4D smooth differentiable manifold; no metric, gauge field, or connection structure is assumed a priori.
    \item \textbf{\( \gamma: \mathbb{R} \to M \)}: A physical 1D filament (worldline) embedded in \( M \).
    \item \textbf{\( \lambda \)}: Affine parameter along the filament.
    \item \textbf{\( v^\mu = \frac{d\gamma^\mu}{d\lambda} \)}: Tangent vector to filament.
    \item \textbf{\( \Sigma_t \)}: Propagating 3D wavefront (resolving surface) sweeping through \( M \).
    \item \textbf{\( u^\mu(x) \)}: Local time-flow vector field normal to \( \Sigma_t \).
    \item \textbf{\( \theta_4(x) \)}: Misalignment angle between \( v^\mu \) and \( u^\mu \); interpreted as mass-related resistance to propagation.
    \item \textbf{\( S_{\mu\nu} = \nabla_\mu u_\nu + \nabla_\nu u_\mu \)}: Strain tensor encoding foliation curvature and deformation.
    \item \textbf{\( \xi^\mu(\lambda) \)}: Transverse perturbation field on filament \( \gamma \).
    \item \textbf{\( \pi_\mu(\lambda) \)}: Canonical momentum conjugate to \( \xi^\mu(\lambda) \).
    \item \textbf{\( \tau(x) \)}: Local twist or winding sector of filament, encoding charge and quantum flavor.
    \item \textbf{\( \psi(x) \)}: Hypothetical emergent matter field defined by local bundle states of filaments.
    \item \textbf{\( T \)}: Filament tension (Planck-scale baseline energy).
    \item \textbf{\( A \)}: Filament rigidity (determines transverse scale).
    \item \textbf{\( \ell_f = (2A/T)^{1/3} \)}: Emergent transverse scale of filament structure.
    \item \textbf{\( Q \)}: Topological suppression factor (e.g. linking, winding, or knot class).
    \item \textbf{\( m^{(eff)} = \frac{T \ell_f}{c^2 Q} \)}: Suppressed mass for topological excitation.
    \item \textbf{Hopf link / Borromean link}: Topologically stable filament bindings (n=2,3 respectively).
\end{itemize}

\section*{Policy Notes}

\begin{itemize}
    \item One symbol, one meaning: enforced throughout SAT.O framework.
    \item All definitions are native to SAT.4D geometry.
    \item No reversion to legacy physics terminology unless explicitly mapped in supplemental compendium.
\end{itemize}
