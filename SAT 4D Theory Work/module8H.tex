All terms already appear (or are mandated) in Modules~3 \& 5. The scale $\Lambda$ absorbs loop-corrected vacuum energy from the torsion condensate; no new mass scale is introduced explicitly.

\section{Background Dynamics}
For a spatially flat FRW metric, the slow–roll equations are
\begin{align}
3H^2 M_P^2 &\simeq V(\theta),\\
3H\dot{\theta} &\simeq -V'(\theta) \;=\; -\frac{\Lambda^4}{f}\,\sin(\theta/f).
\end{align}

Define the slow--roll parameters
\[
\epsilon \;=\; \frac12 \bigl(V'/V\bigr)^2 M_P^2,
\qquad
\eta \;=\; \frac{V''}{V} M_P^2.
\]
Inflation ends when $\max(\epsilon,|\eta|)\approx1$.

\section{Observable Predictions}
The scalar spectral tilt and the tensor-to-scalar ratio are
\[
n_s \;=\; 1 - 6\epsilon_* + 2\eta_*,
\qquad
r \;=\; 16\epsilon_*,
\]
where $*$ denotes evaluation roughly $55$ e-folds before the end of inflation.\\[4pt]
For $\theta_* \approx \pi$ one finds the analytic approximation
\[
\epsilon_* \;\simeq\; \frac{1}{2N_*^2}\left(\frac{M_P}{f}\right)^2
\;\;\Longrightarrow\;\;
r \;\simeq\; \frac{8}{N_*^2}\left(\frac{M_P}{f}\right)^2.
\]
With $N_*=55$,
\[
\hat f \ge 5 \;\Longrightarrow\; r \le 0.03,
\]
comfortably inside the Planck~PR4 $+$ BK18 bound, and the corresponding $n_s \approx 0.965$.
