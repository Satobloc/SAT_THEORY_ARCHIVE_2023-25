\section*{Purpose}

This document defines the deployment constraints and interpretation modes available within the SAT.4D framework. It outlines the structural calculus used to model emergent physics from filamentary 4D geometry, and governs the allowed reasoning frames in both core and extended theory construction.

\section*{Interpretive Modes}

\subsection*{Mode 1: True Block (Core Mode)}

\begin{itemize}
    \item The 4D manifold \( M \) is fully resolved and fixed.
    \item All motion, causality, and change are projections from filament structure intersected by a propagating 3D surface \( \Sigma_t \).
    \item All SAT.O modules must default to Mode 1 unless otherwise stated.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Mode 2: Semi-Dynamic Block}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Allows localized, constrained adjustments of filamentary structure at points of high strain or entanglement.
    \item May be useful for modeling quantum collapse, decoherence, or entropy flow.
    \item Must be flagged and used only in non-core derivations or simulations.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Mode 3: Solidification Front}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Treats \( \Sigma_t \) as a physical crystallization or decoherence front resolving future structure.
    \item May be used in cosmology, entropy models, or interpretations of the arrow of time.
    \item Strictly prohibited from appearing in falsifiability-grounded SAT.O modules.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Structural Calculus Primer}

SAT recasts all physics as static geometry on a 4D block. Structural relationships include:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Filament} \( \gamma^\mu(\lambda) \): A real 1D path through spacetime.
    \item \textbf{Time Vector Field} \( u^\mu(x) \): Normal to \( \Sigma_t \); used for projection.
    \item \textbf{Misalignment Angle} \( \theta_4(x) \): Angle between \( v^\mu \) and \( u^\mu \), indicating resistance to propagation.
    \item \textbf{Strain Tensor} \( S_{\mu\nu} = \nabla_\mu u_\nu + \nabla_\nu u_\mu \): Encodes curvature and energetic response.
    \item \textbf{Topological Structures}: Links, knots, windings encode charge, flavor, and binding.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Working Heuristics}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Nothing evolves}; everything is already embedded in 4D.
    \item \textbf{All energy is strain}; all motion is intersection.
    \item \textbf{Mass is kink resistance}, not intrinsic substance.
    \item \textbf{Fields are topological classes} of filament configurations.
    \item \textbf{ψ(x)} emerges from local averaging of twist, tension, and phase-lock state.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Usage Guidelines}

\begin{itemize}
    \item All formal derivations must state their interpretive mode.
    \item Mixing interpretive modes within a single derivation is not permitted.
    \item Interpretive overlays (Modes 2 or 3) are allowed only in marked speculative or cosmological modules.
\end{itemize}
