Canonical Quantization of the SAT Field Sector
I.
CANONICAL QUANTIZATION OF THE SAT
FIELD SECTOR
The derivation of the quantum properties of the
Scalar–Angular Theory (SAT) proceeds through the
canonical quantization approach applied to the contin-
uous field degrees of freedom [1]. The formulation relies
on the canonical commutation relations established for
the field and its conjugate momentum over the continu-
ous sector [2].
A.
The General Canonical Formalism
The
dynamics
of
the
SAT
continuous
fields
(θµ, uµ, Jµν) [3],
governed by the total Lagrangian
density Ltotal [4], require the definition of canonical
conjugate momenta π corresponding to the generalized
fields ξ [5].
For the gravitational component of the SAT frame-
work, the Hamiltonian density HGR+SAT is obtained
via a Legendre transform, resulting in the Hamiltonian
and momentum constraints (H0, H∗
i ) expressed using the
ADM lapse N and shift N i variables [6]. This approach
involves the canonical momentum πij conjugate to the
spatial metric h∗
ij [7].
B.
Canonical Quantization Condition
In the general continuous sector of SAT, canonical
quantization proceeds through the definition of symbolic
commutators [1].
If ξ(λ) represents a continuous field
component and π(λ′) represents its canonical conjugate
momentum [5], the quantization condition is given by the
commutation relation:
[ξ(λ), π(λ′)] = iℏδ(λ −λ′) .
(1)
This relation establishes the standard quantization con-
dition over the continuous sector of the theory [2]. The
relationship holds over the continuous variable λ, signify-
ing that the commutator is non-zero only when the field
locations coincide (λ = λ′) [2, 5].
