5.5
Diagnostic Implications
Pure topology (w, τ) is insufficient. A third term is required:
mℓ= βw + γτ + δf,

duce me, mµ, mτ within < 10%.
Falsifiability.
If no combination of (w, τ, δf) can match the lepton spectrum without ad
hoc flavour parameters, SAT’s claim of geometric mass generation for leptons fails.
