C.
Handling Constraints and the Gravitational
Sector
The full functional integral for SAT must address the
constraints arising from the time-flow vector uµ and the
gravitational interaction gµν.
1.
Constrained Time-Flow Field
The vector field uµ is dynamically normalized via the
constraint C1 : uµuµ + 1 = 0, enforced classically by a
Lagrange multiplier λ(x). In the path integral formula-
tion, this constraint must be handled, typically by inte-
grating out the multiplier λ or choosing a specific gauge
fixing for the timelike field uµ. The associated gravita-
tional Lagrangian Lgrav already includes this multiplier
term.
2.
Gravitational Constraints
When treating the metric gµν as a field to be quantized
(as required for Quantum Gravity effects), the integra-
tion D[g] must account for the spacetime diffeomorphism
invariance.
Using the ADM decomposition, the gravi-
tational Hamiltonian density HGR+SAT is expressed in
terms of the lapse (N) and shift (N i) functions and the
constraints (H0, Hi):
HGR+SAT = NH0 + N iHi + λ(uµuµ + 1).
(4)
These Hamiltonian (H0) and momentum (Hi) con-
straints involve the canonical momentum πij conjugate

The full path integral over the combined fields D[ξ, g]
thus requires employing the Faddeev–Popov procedure
(or other gauge fixing methods) to properly integrate over
the constraints H0 and Hi, ensuring only the physical
degrees of freedom contribute to Z.
