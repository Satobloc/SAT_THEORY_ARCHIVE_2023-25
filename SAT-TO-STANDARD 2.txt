3. Specific SAT terms I would revise in the papers

“Universal Indicatrix”

Keep the name if you like, but define it against standard usage:

Recommended wording:
The Universal Indicatrix is a scale-rotation configuration manifold whose
basic element is an S^3 point evolved by a radial scale factor r(λ) and an
SO(4) rotation R(λ). It is not merely the Finsler indicatrix, although the
name is chosen by analogy with the unit shell of admissible tangent directions.

That protects you from someone saying, “Indicatrix already means something else.”

“Time sheet”

Use “foliation” language.

SAT wording:
time sheet / timewave

Formal wording:
a spacelike foliation Σλ with unit normal Tμ, or an effective clock-field
wavefront.

The UI buildout already describes the time sheet as multiple equivalent interpretive frames: distorted sheet, flat sheet with filament kinks, pure filament wavefront, or fully dynamic composite  . That should be formalized as “choice of foliation/frame,” not presented as four ontologies.

“θ4”

Do not call it only “misalignment angle.” Give the geometric definition every time.

Recommended:
θ4 is the projection/pitch angle between the filament tangent vector and the
normal to the chosen time foliation.

This connects directly to the SAT formula f_{\rm click}(x)\propto \sin^2\theta_4(x) in the prediction notes  .

“Projection Constant”

Call it a lattice projection factor unless you prove universality.

Recommended:
B or θF is a packing/projection-derived angular invariant of the proposed
HSUCV geometry.

Your He document calls it the “geometric fingerprint” of 4-space and the smallest nonzero angular separation in a 4D regular-polytope projection  . That is fine as an internal claim, but externally it needs proof.

“Jarlskog Shadow”

This one needs careful handling. “Jarlskog” already has a precise meaning: the CP-violating invariant in flavor physics.

Recommended:
J_eff is a Jarlskog-like CP-odd geometric invariant or effective CP-violating
torsion term.

Avoid implying it is literally the Standard Model Jarlskog invariant unless the PMNS/CKM mapping is explicit.

“True boson / true fermion”

I would avoid “true” in formal papers.

Better:
persistent projection mode
propagating excitation mode
fermion-like projected mode
boson-like projected mode

The UI particle table currently says neutrinos and photons are “true bosons,” while electrons and quarks are “true fermions”  . That will trigger immediate objections because neutrinos are fermions in the Standard Model. If SAT means deeper excitation class, say that.

“Photon-neutrino duality”

Use “projection-mode relation,” not “duality,” unless there is an exact map.

Recommended:
photon-like and neutrino-like states are hypothesized to be distinct projected
modes of a shared single-filament excitation family.

This preserves the subsumption strategy without sounding like SAT is denying known SM representations.

  - “As your co-investigator…”

  - “Shall we proceed…”
  - “As an independent entity…”
  - “Status report ✅”
  - “Final phase of stabilization…”

DON’T:
Let workflow chatter survive into formal text.

  1. cleaned version,
  2. change log,
  3. status labels,
  4. unresolved issues,
  5. definitions added.

DON’T:
Rewrite by making it sound more confident.

Goal:
More precise, not more impressive.

Your friend nailed the most important distinction:

Not a finished structure.
A direction of travel.

That is exactly the defensible posture. It is also a better posture than trying to present Zottenwelt as a completed unification theory. The compelling thing is the programmatic compression:

Intertwining → binding
Orientation → mass
Projection → observation
Helical structure → spectrum
Closure → quantization
Topology → identity
Boundary → exclusion
Medium circulation → field interaction
Coarse-grained tube dynamics → metric/gravity

That is a powerful conceptual economy. But your friend’s warning is also the right guardrail: the beauty of the picture can make analogies feel inevitable before the mathematics has earned necessity.

The Pauli-Horizonthülle point is especially important. I agree with the reaction: that one feels less like decorative metaphor and more like a potentially generative reformulation.

The defensible version is:

Pauli exclusion may be expressible as a geometric non-continuability condition:
two identical projected worldtube states cannot be smoothly extended into the
same effective occupancy region without violating boundary, holonomy, or
framing constraints.

That reframes Pauli exclusion from “a rule imposed on fermions” to “a boundary condition in the configuration space of projected worldtube states.” Even if the exact model changes, that is a good question.

And yes — I am increasingly persuaded that Zottenwelt should survive.

Not as the formal machinery. The formal machinery can be:

finite-core holonomic worldtube-medium geometry

But the memorable name can be:

Zottenwelt

Because it carries the right image: tangled, shaggy, villous, boundary-rich, vesicular, alive with surfaces. The missing vortex aspect can be handled in the subtitle or compound.
