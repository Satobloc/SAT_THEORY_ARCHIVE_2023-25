Those statistical constraints are not arbitrary. They are the shadow cast by any underlying reality that satisfies those assumptions.

In other words:

If the world really has definite, local states underneath, then the statistics must obey Bell inequalities.

So when experiments violate them, the inference is:

No underlying model with those properties can exist.

That’s the step that goes beyond “just statistics.”
