## Descriptive content clusters

### Universal Indicatrix / coordinate-generator materials

Documents in this group include `UI CONFIGURATION (nolat).pdf`, `Universal Indicatrix in my words CHARACTERIZATION` from the uploaded session, `DERIVATIVE INDICATRIX (nolat).pdf`, `WHIRLIGIG SUMMARY (nolat).pdf` from the uploaded session, and related synthesis files. These materials concern a rotating/scaling 4D coordinate generator, often expressed as a relation of scale and rotation acting on an initial point on a hypersphere.

Recurring terms include Universal Indicatrix, Whirligig, scale control, rotation control, SO(4), six planes of rotation, hypersphere, superhelical path, and projection/trajectory mapping.

### Superhelical and particle-classification materials

Documents in this group include `SAT PARTICLE ZOO LAGRANGIAN (nolattice).pdf`, `BARYGENESIS (nolat).pdf`, `Photoneutrino Sector (nolat).pdf`, `Orders and Q numbers.pdf`, and `STANDARD ATOM (nolat).pdf`. These materials discuss nth-order superhelical filaments, persistent versus transient coiling, Q/order counting, braid structures, leptons/quarks/mesons/baryons, and possible photon/neutrino sector relationships.

Recurring terms include persistent coil, transient excitation, true boson, true fermion, Q number, coiling order, Hopf link, Borromean triplet, braid smoothing, pseudomass, and theta_4.

### Unit-cell / toy Lagrangian materials

Documents in this group include `UNIT CELL.pdf` and `VARIOUS LAGRANGIANS (speculative).pdf` from the uploaded session, and related root-level materials where present. These materials appear to develop a ladder from 2D overlapping circles to 3D overlapping spheres and 4D overlapping hyperspheres or 24-cell unit-cell descriptions.

Recurring terms include kinetic term, potential/overlap term, resistance term, rotational term, overlap area/volume/hypervolume, unit cell, 24-cell, and hypersphere lattice.

### Relativity / quantum / sector-bridge materials

Documents in this group include `Relativistic–Quantum Isomorphism (nolat).pdf`, `4DHH LAGRANGIAN (nolat).pdf`, `QUANTUM GRAVITYFOAM (nolat).pdf`, `GRAVITATIONAL NANOSTRUCTURE (Nolat).pdf`, and `MARCH 12 SYNTHESIS (nolat).pdf`. These materials discuss possible relationships among relativistic curvature, quantum spectra, superhelical worldline dynamics, coarse-graining, and topological mode density.

Recurring terms include co-metric, metric emergence, Laplace-Beltrami eigenvalues, coarse-graining scale, topological mode density, Universal Winding, gravity-gauge transform, and collective attenuation.

### Pulsar/glitch and publication-branch materials

Documents in this group include `GLITCH (nolat).pdf`, `PULSAR GLITCH (Nolat).pdf`, `ST-QM-GR-SM (nolat).pdf`, `PHYS (nolat).pdf`, and `MINKOWSKI PROPER.pdf`. These materials appear to present or draft sector-specific applications, often around pulsar glitches, metric continuity, relativistic rotation, and publication-facing terminology.

Recurring terms include pulsar glitch, critical velocity, metric continuity, Achromatic Phase Snap, geometric corner, projection boundary, luminosity/gauge coupling change, and Minkowski worldline diagrams.

### Closure / compression materials

Documents in this group include `CORE PACK.pdf`, `CORE PACK 2.pdf`, and `FINAL_CLOSURE.pdf`. These materials appear to compress formulas, constants, and sector relationships from the surrounding working-document packet into compact reference or prompt-like forms.
