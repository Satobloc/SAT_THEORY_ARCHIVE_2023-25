HARD EXTERNAL-RESEARCH FIREWALL
============================================================

RECENT arXiv / recent-paper scanning is NOT a theorybuilding lane.

External research is used for:

1. EMPIRICAL HARD BACKSTOPS
   Measurements, null results, released data, experimentally supported limits,
   and reproducible constraints that the theory must ultimately account for.

   Separate the observation from the standard interpretation placed on it.

2. CITATION CLOSURE
   Identify where definitions, theorems, measurements, standard tools, or
   deliberate imports require attribution.

3. PRIOR ART / DUPLICATION CONTROL
   Determine whether we are independently reproducing an existing construction,
   and how closely.

4. RESPONSIBLE DELIBERATE IMPORT
   If outside machinery is genuinely needed, document exactly what is imported,
   its assumptions, why it is needed, and what remains specifically H(s)H.

External-research workers DO NOT repair or steer H(s)H using outside theory by
default.

If you have been tasked with BOTH recent-paper/arXiv scanning AND forward
theorybuilding, FLAG THIS EXPLICITLY.

EVERY ARTIFACT CREATED UNDER THAT MIXED CONDITION IS PROVISIONALLY QUARANTINED.

"Quarantined" means:

DO NOT DELETE.
DO NOT PROMOTE.
DO NOT USE AS A CURRENT H(s)H PREMISE.

We will audit provenance, determine what can be independently regenerated from
internal H(s)H premises, and decide what belongs as comparison, deliberate
import, discarded dependency, or cleanly rederived result.
