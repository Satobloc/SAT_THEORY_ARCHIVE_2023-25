## Review status legend

- CLOSED: derivation appears prospective, explicit, independently normalized, and empirically matched.
- OPEN-PROMISING: clear prediction and observable, but not adequately tested or fully audited.
- UNDERDEFINED: interesting relation, but missing a unique measurement map, tolerance, or dependency graph.
- FRACTURED: current derivation is internally inconsistent, materially misses established data, or depends on a repair term not yet derived.
- HISTORICAL / SUPERSEDED: useful provenance, but not current formulation.
- UNKNOWN: not enough source inspection yet.
