SAT Predictions and Retrodictions (with Estimated Tolerances)
=============================================================

1. Quantized Neutron Star Glitch Sizes
   - Prediction: Glitch magnitudes (Δν) in neutron stars may exhibit preferred values or quantized steps.
   - Tolerance: ~10⁻⁷ to 10⁻⁶ Hz (Δν)
   - Status: Look for clustering in glitch size histograms across consistent populations.

2. Directional Modulation of Glitch Magnitudes
   - Prediction: Apparent glitch sizes will vary systematically by sky direction and distance due to inertial anisotropy.
   - Tolerance: Modulation on the order of ~1–10% across large sky sectors.
   - Status: Requires spatially clustered glitching pulsars.

3. Sidereal Period Modulation in Nuclear Decay or Neutron Lifetime
   - Prediction: Decay rates or neutron lifetimes may exhibit daily (~23.934 hr) oscillations.
   - Tolerance: 1–10 ppm amplitude modulation.
   - Status: Look for time-of-day correlations in lifetime measurements.

4. Drift in Frequency Ratios Between Atomic Clocks
   - Prediction: Tiny secular drift in frequency ratios (optical vs. microwave) due to anisotropic inertial structure.
   - Tolerance: ~10⁻¹⁷ per year
   - Status: Compare long-term clock stability across different atomic species.

5. Non-Reciprocal Optical Rotation in Chiral Media
   - Prediction: Light exhibits forward-backward asymmetry in polarization rotation in chiral samples without external fields.
   - Tolerance: ~10⁻⁴ to 10⁻³ deg/mm
   - Status: Compare rotation in forward vs. reverse propagation through enantiopure materials.

6. Directional Dependence in Gravitational Wave Arrival Times
   - Prediction: Gravitational waves may arrive earlier or later than expected depending on propagation direction.
   - Tolerance: ~0.1–1 ms variation
   - Status: Analyze GW events from different sky directions for systematic residuals.

7. Longitude-Dependent Deviation in Foucault Pendulum Precession
   - Prediction: Precession rate deviates slightly from Earth-rotation model depending on longitude.
   - Tolerance: ~0.1–1 arcsecond/day
   - Status: Requires synchronized pendulums across longitudinal stations.

8. Sky-Dependent Variability in Inferred Orbital Parameters
   - Prediction: Inferred parameters (e.g., Keplerian orbital period) may shift depending on system orientation.
   - Tolerance: Biases of ~10⁻⁶ fractional error across hemispheres
   - Status: Compare binary systems with similar parameters in opposite sky sectors.

9. Direction-Dependent Redshift Residuals in Quasars
   - Prediction: After accounting for cosmological redshift, residuals will have anisotropic structure.
   - Tolerance: Δz ~ 10⁻⁵–10⁻⁴
   - Status: Requires large uniform sky sample and statistical anisotropy analysis.

10. Preferred Angular Separation Between Repeaters (e.g., FRBs)
    - Prediction: Some angular spacings between repeaters or glitching sources will occur more often than random.
    - Tolerance: Peak signal at specific angular offsets (tens of degrees)
    - Status: Spatial autocorrelation function of glitching/frb sources.
