
SAT: PREDICTIONS AND RETRODICTIONS — SUMMARY LIST
=================================================

This document contains a concise, critic-grade list of proposed predictions and retrodictions that follow from the SAT_OLDSCHOOL framework. Each includes approximate tolerance requirements where relevant.

I. PREDICTIONS
--------------

1. CLOCK DRIFT (SAT Timing Deviation)
   - Prediction: SAT predicts drift in timekeeping compared to General Relativity in regions with filament density variation.
   - Test: GPS satellites and atomic clocks in varying orbital altitudes.
   - Tolerance: Predicted deviation ~10^-14 to 10^-16 seconds per day. Detectable with next-gen timing arrays.

2. DOMAIN WALL PHASE INTERFERENCE
   - Prediction: Interference effects due to domain-like structures in emergent foliation.
   - Test: Large-scale quantum interferometry or precision laser arrays.
   - Tolerance: Phase shift of ~10^-4 to 10^-6 radians. Requires baseline coherence over ~10 meters or more.

3. PULSAR TIMING ANOMALIES
   - Prediction: Minute deviations from GR in pulsar signal residuals due to geometric transitions in filament congruence.
   - Test: Long-term pulsar timing arrays (e.g. NANOGrav, IPTA).
   - Tolerance: Signal drift or jitter at 100 ns to 1 μs over years.

4. COSMIC CLOCK BIAS (Cosmological Redshift Drift)
   - Prediction: Apparent redshift evolution slightly modified due to SAT time foliation dynamics.
   - Test: Deep cosmological surveys, baryon acoustic oscillations (BAO) time dependence.
   - Tolerance: Δz ~ 10^-5 over decade-scale time baselines.

5. PARTICLE DECAY STABILITY ANOMALIES
   - Prediction: Certain decay channels (e.g., neutron, mesons) exhibit suppressed or modulated rates due to topological interference.
   - Test: Long-baseline neutrino decay experiments, meson factories.
   - Tolerance: Deviations on the order of 10^-6 from Standard Model predictions.

6. NONLINEAR WAVEFRONT SHIFTS IN HIGH-FIELD REGIMES
   - Prediction: In strong EM or gravitational fields, wave propagation deviates subtly from GR predictions.
   - Test: Near magnetars, black holes, or using high-field laser systems.
   - Tolerance: Light path deviation on the order of pico-radians over ~1 AU.

7. TOPOLOGICAL SPIN-STATISTICS VIOLATIONS (EDGE CASE)
   - Prediction: Rare violations of spin-statistics in high-topological-curvature domains.
   - Test: Rare event detectors, ultra-cold fermionic condensates.
   - Tolerance: 1 in 10^12 events, or rarer.

II. RETRODICTIONS
-----------------

1. PROTON STABILITY
   - Retrodiction: Proton remains stable due to Z₃ topological twist; decay channels forbidden by topology.
   - Match: Experimental lower bound τₚ > 10^34 yrs.
   - Tolerance: ✔ Consistent with data.

2. NEUTRINO OSCILLATION PARITY
   - Retrodiction: Geometry-induced parity bias in flavor oscillations.
   - Match: Small deviations in neutrino mixing angles and CP violation phase.
   - Tolerance: Within current experimental bounds.

3. BLACK HOLE SINGULARITY AVOIDANCE
   - Retrodiction: No singularities — instead, dense filamentary knotting resolves curvature.
   - Match: Suggestive with Penrose-like structure avoidance; testable indirectly.
   - Tolerance: No hard data yet.

4. COSMOLOGICAL CONSTANT (Λ)
   - Retrodiction: Apparent vacuum energy arises from net topological tension of vacuum filament network.
   - Match: Order-of-magnitude agreement with observed Λ ~ 10^-122 (Planck units).
   - Tolerance: Off by factor ≤ 10⁰ to 10¹. Needs refinement.

5. DISCRETIZATION OF CHARGE
   - Retrodiction: Electric charge quantized due to filament winding number and holonomy class.
   - Match: Predicts integer/electron charge units.
   - Tolerance: ✔ Matches observation.

6. UNIFICATION SCALE HINTS (IMPLICIT)
   - Retrodiction: Convergence of interaction strengths at topological saturation scale (not a unification in group-theoretic sense).
   - Match: Suggestive alignment with GUT scales (~10^15–10^16 GeV).
   - Tolerance: Plausible order-of-magnitude; no precision derivation yet.

III. PREDICTIVE CLAIM STATUS
----------------------------
✔ = Matches existing data (retrodiction).
❓ = Testable but unconfirmed.
⚠️ = Requires extreme precision or new instrumentation.
