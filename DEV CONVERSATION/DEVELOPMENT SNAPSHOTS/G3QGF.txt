Scalar–Angular–Twist (SAT) / Spacetime As Topology (SAT) Framework
Status Overview — August 2025

I. Conceptual Foundation
SAT proposes that all physical reality emerges from one-dimensional filaments embedded in a 4D manifold.
- No fundamental fields or forces are assumed.
- Geometry and topology of filament bundles generate matter, interactions, and spacetime itself.
- A resolving surface Σt propagates through the 4D substrate; intersections with filaments generate observable 3D physics.

1. Filaments: Basic constituents with hyperhelical embeddings, defined by phase angles and tension.
2. Topological Binding:

3. Emergent Geometry:

4. Gauge Symmetries:

5. Mass Generation:
   - Mass scales suppressed by topological invariants Q (linking + winding + writhe).
   - Effective mass meff = m0/Q; ensures no stable particles above Q=3.
