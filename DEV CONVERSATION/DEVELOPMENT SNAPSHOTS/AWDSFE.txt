SAT Project — State of Play
(as of 2025-08-31)

1) Executive Summary
• SAT has matured from a conceptual framework into a testable, mode-synchronized program with clear falsifiability gates. Recent documents finalize the link between topology (winding/linking, θ4, τ, Sμν) and observed spectra, decay widths, and emergent gauge/gravity structure.
• The mass sector (O9–O10) reproduces light-flavour masses within ≲6% without retuning successful baryon/heavy-meson fits. The decay-width sector (O12) closes a prior phenomenology gap with a minimal, topology-conditioned law. The flavour sector (O13) adds a discrete A4 embedding that explains charged-lepton hierarchy and PMNS structure with no new continuous parameters.
• Modes have been redeployed with sharper responsibilities, enabling rigorous hand-offs: Mecha/Sim/Viz/Pheno feed structured outputs to Track; Proof/MathProof pin down formal derivations.

2) Architecture & Modes (operational view)
• Foundations: Onto, Dict, Axiom formalize the primitives (u^μ, θ4, ψ, τ), crosswalks to GR/QFT/SM, and core principles.
• Design & Mechanics: Mecha constructs Lagrangians/Hamiltonians and constraint closures; Sim discretizes dynamics and runs evolutions; Bridge maps SAT structures onto SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1); Viz generates reproducible figures.
• Empirics & Quantification: Pheno defines observables and falsifiability; Track consolidates status and outputs; Quant & Prism handle quantization and spectral decomposition.
• Mathematical Validation: Proof (logical) and MathProof (symbolic) separate correctness from calculation.
• Perspective: Critic stress-tests assumptions; Insight crafts intuitive models; Sage integrates the whole system.

3) Module Progress (high level)
• O0/O0-A: Foundations sanity pass, dimensional ledger, and “Mode ladder” (True Block → Triggered Dynamics → Crystallisation). Lower modes carry core derivations; Mode 2 adds controlled corrections for GR matching; Mode 3 is long-horizon conceptual closure.
• O9–O10 (Mass pipeline): Topology-anchored mapping from 4D string-like excitations to spectra. The extended mass law m(Q, τ_χ) = (m0/Q)·exp[−γ_χ τ_χ] fits π, μ, K, τ and leaves baryon success intact. Deliverables include a Lean proof (torsion uniqueness), numeric notebook, and human-readable report.
• O12 (Decay widths): Minimal law for Q=2 bundle decays, Γ ∝ (p/p0)^{2L+1}·exp[−α_top^2·ΔE]·exp[−β(N_disc−1)], encoding the OZI rule as a strict filament-counting factor. Depends only on O1 and O5; introduces at most one universal dimensionless constant.
• O13 (Discrete flavour): Embeds charged-lepton filaments in A4 with “no new reals,” yielding hierarchy and PMNS structure from discrete phase factors. Adds logical updates to earlier modules (O5/O6/O8/O11) while remaining independent of O12.
