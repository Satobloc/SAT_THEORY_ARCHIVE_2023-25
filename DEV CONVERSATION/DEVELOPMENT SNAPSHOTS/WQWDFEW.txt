Scalar–Angular–Twist (SAT) Framework — Comprehensive Overview (August 2025)
============================================================================

I. Core Ontology and Fields
----------------------------
SAT reimagines physics using a filament + time-wavefront ontology. It moves away from traditional fields-in-spacetime by introducing three fundamental fields:

1. θ₄(x): Real scalar field of angular misalignment
   - Governs mass, inertia, and optical asymmetries
   - Effective mass: m ∝ sin²(θ₄)
   - Z₃ symmetric potential: V(θ₄) = −μ² cos(3θ₄)
   - Three degenerate vacua → domain walls with topological tension

2. uᵘ(x): Dynamical time-wavefront vector field
   - Unit timelike vector field: uᵘ u_ᵤ = −1
   - Encodes preferred foliation and time propagation
   - Kinetic term: α (∇ₘuⁿ)(∇ₘuₙ), often compared to Einstein-æther models
   - May mimic gravity via curvature and vorticity

3. τ ∈ ℤ₃: Discrete topological twist variable
   - Encodes Z₃ fusion algebra: τᵢ + τⱼ + τₖ ≡ 0 mod 3
   - Governs defect clustering and topological selection rules
   - Modeled via lattice fusion penalties or cohomological BF terms
   - Still non-dynamical in current formulations
