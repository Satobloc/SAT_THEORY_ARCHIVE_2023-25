I. Empirical Predictions (Prospective, Testable)
-------------------------------------------------

1. Optical Phase Shift from θ₄ Domain Walls
   - Prediction: Δφ ≈ 0.125 radians
   - Tolerance: ±0.015 rad
   - Test: Birefringent multilayer stack (photonic stack experiment)

2. Mass Anisotropy from uᵘ Curvature
   - Prediction: Direction-dependent mass/inertia in torsion balance
   - Tolerance: Δm/m ≈ 10⁻⁶ to 10⁻⁷
   - Test: Rotating torsion pendulum or frame-sensitive oscillator

3. Nonreciprocal Optical Transmission
   - Prediction: Directional asymmetry in transmission coefficients (ΔT ≈ 0.05–0.1)
   - Tolerance: ≥5σ above standard waveplate or metamaterial models
   - Test: Optical diode behavior in kinked media

4. STM Defect Clustering in Moiré Systems
   - Prediction: Suppression of τ₁–τ₁–τ₁ triplet junctions (Z₃ forbidden)
   - Tolerance: Deviation from Poisson baseline by ≥30%
   - Test: STM/LDOS analysis on TBG or TMD heterostructures

5. Variation in Fine Structure Constant (Δα)
   - Prediction: Δα/α ≈ 10⁻⁵ over cosmological baselines
   - Tolerance: Match to dipole or drift trend within ±1σ of Webb dataset
   - Test: Cross-match quasar absorption lines with θ₄ gradients

6. GPS Clock Drift (Preferred Frame Effect)
   - Prediction: Timing drift correlated with uᵘ directionality
   - Tolerance: <5 ns per orbit deviation from GR-corrected models
   - Test: Analysis of GPS archival and high-resolution logs

7. Planetary Limb Polarization Asymmetry
   - Prediction: Differential polarization at limb edges due to θ₄ gradient
   - Tolerance: ≥2% deviation in polarization angle between hemispheres
   - Test: Mars/Venus limb scan comparison, multi-wavelength

8. Geophysical Rupture Front Asymmetry
   - Prediction: Systematic orientation bias in fault ruptures
   - Tolerance: ≥10° deviation from isotropic rupture model
   - Test: Global seismic dataset analysis
