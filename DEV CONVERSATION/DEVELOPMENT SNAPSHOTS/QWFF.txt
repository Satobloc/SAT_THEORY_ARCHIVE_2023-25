SAT Predictions and Retrodictions
=================================

Note: All tolerances refer to maximum deviation from SAT expectations before falsifiability is triggered.

1. Particle Stability Limits (SAT.O4)
------------------------------------
Prediction: Only Q = 1 (neutrinos), Q = 2 (mesons), Q = 3 (baryons) are stable filament bundles.
Tolerance: Any confirmed long-lived Q ≥ 4 hadron (e.g. stable tetraquark) falsifies SAT.

2. Mass Suppression by Topological Charge (SAT.O3/O8)
-----------------------------------------------------
Prediction: Effective mass is suppressed as m_eff = m0 / Q.
Tolerance: Particle mass ratios should deviate < 15% from ideal ratios (1, 1/2, 1/3) unless explained by known geometric corrections.

3. Gauge Coupling Ratios from Topology (SAT.O5)
-----------------------------------------------
Prediction: g_U1 : g_SU2 : g_SU3 ∝ sqrt(ρ_wind) : sqrt(ρ_link) : sqrt(ρ_triplet).
Tolerance: Coupling ratios within 20–30% of experimental PDG values.

4. Gravity as Bounded Strain (SAT.O2/O6)
----------------------------------------
Prediction: Emergent metric reproduces Newtonian gravity with UV-regularized core Φ(r) = –GM / sqrt(r² + ε²).
Tolerance: Deviations from GR must be < 0.01% at r > 10⁻¹⁴ m; potential flattening at smaller r is allowed.

5. Jet Suppression in QGP (SAT.O7/O8)
-------------------------------------
Prediction: Jet quenching in heavy-ion collisions shows discrete energy loss tied to filament Q-class.
Tolerance: Deviations from continuous energy loss must show evidence of quantization in Q = 2–3 bins.

6. Absence of Free Quarks/Gluons (SAT.O3)
-----------------------------------------
Prediction: Deconfined QCD states in QGP are transient unbraided filaments, not free fields.
Tolerance: No evidence of stable, unbound color charges in QGP at any scale.

7. Neutrino Masses and Q = 1 Simplicity (SAT.O3/O8)
----------------------------------------------------
Prediction: Neutrinos are Q = 1 filaments, near-massless unless twisted or defect-laden.
Tolerance: m_ν values must remain suppressed (m < 0.1 eV) unless topological anomalies are included.

8. Quantized Mass Spectrum (SAT.O9/O10)
---------------------------------------
Prediction: Particle mass spectrum follows m² ∝ Q² with sector-specific slope/intercept from compactification topology.
Tolerance: RMS error in fitted mass spectrum < 10% across leptons and light hadrons.

9. Filament Strain Drives Curvature (SAT.O2/O6)
-----------------------------------------------
Prediction: Einstein field equations emerge statistically from filament strain tensor S_{μν}.
Tolerance: Metric derivations must reproduce GR to within < 1% in known limits.

10. No Singularities (SAT.O2/O6)
-------------------------------
Prediction: All gravitational configurations are regularized by ε ∼ ℓ_f/π.
Tolerance: No physical model in SAT allows curvature singularities.

11. Gravitational Wave Echoes from Topological Mergers (SAT.O6/O7)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Prediction: Mergers of high-Q bundles (black holes) may show echoes, damping asymmetries, or quantized ringdown.
Tolerance: Observation of smooth, classical GR-only waveforms is allowed; distinct echoes would favor SAT.

12. Time is Emergent from Slicing (SAT.O7)
------------------------------------------
Prediction: Temporal ordering arises from intersection of Σ_t with filament structure.
Tolerance: No falsifiable prediction here—interpretive claim only.
