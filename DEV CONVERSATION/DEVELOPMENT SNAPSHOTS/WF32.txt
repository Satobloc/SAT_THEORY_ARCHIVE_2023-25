SAT — WHERE IT STANDS TODAY
Date: August 31, 2025

====================================================
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
====================================================
SAT (Scalar–Angular / Spacetime-as-Topology) models the universe as a 4D manifold filled with
tensioned 1D filaments. Geometry, fields and particles are emergent properties of the filament
ensemble. The quantum theory uses a curvature-suppressed path integral, which the program
claims removes UV divergences without counterterms. Concrete progress: emergent gravity with
a regularised Newton potential, qualitative/semiquantitative gauge-coupling hierarchies, a
topological S-matrix blueprint, and a falsifiability toolkit. Key gaps: no published explicit
scattering amplitudes yet, incomplete running-coupling derivations, and open issues in the
fermion mass sector. Peer review and community adoption remain limited.

====================================================
1) WHAT SAT IS (ONE-LINER)
====================================================
A background-independent 4D filament theory: spacetime metric, forces and matter arise from
statistics and topology of tensioned, oriented 1D filaments with internal phases.

====================================================
2) CORE FORMAL STRUCTURE (WHAT’S DEFINED)
====================================================
• Axioms: Filamental space (1D filaments in 4D), emergent metric g_{μν} from local filament
  tangents, canonical quantization of filament vibrations, and bundle observables.
• Quantum dynamics: Path integral over filament shapes with exponential curvature penalty
  Z = ∫ Dγ exp[-(T/2) ∫ ||γ̈||² dλ].
• Interactions: Local reconnection and deformation events; “topological S-matrix” maps initial
  to final bundle topologies via sequences of reconnections.
• Topological renormalization: Running and scales tied to flows of linking/winding densities.
(Primary sources: SAT_FULL_BUILDOUT Phase V–VIII; SAT_4D_LEGACY_REFERENCE.)

====================================================
3) THE “BUILT-IN DAMPING” (UV FINITENESS CLAIM)
====================================================
• Mechanism: The curvature-suppressed measure exp[-(T/2)∫||γ̈||²] weights smooth curves and
  exponentially suppresses high-curvature (UV) configurations.
• Claimed consequences: No UV divergences; no counterterms; RG flow arises from integrating
  out small-scale bundle loops; loop observables (g–2, Lamb shift, running α) remain finite.
(Status: asserted at the framework level; explicit loop-by-loop amplitude tables not yet published.)

====================================================
4) RESULTS TO DATE (QUANTITATIVE SIGNALS)
====================================================
GRAVITY (WEAK-FIELD)
• Emergent metric from filament ensembles reproduces a regularised Newton potential
  Φ(r) = -GM / sqrt(r² + ε²) with ε ~ 10^(-20) m from lattice fits.
• Deviation from 1/r is < 10^(-4)% for r ≳ 10^(-14) m → consistent with current tests.
• Gravity–gauge hierarchy reproduced at the order-of-magnitude / factor-of-2 level without
  adjustable parameters (via rare embedding-mode densities).

GAUGE COUPLINGS
• Topological counts give g1 < g2 < g3 automatically.
• Ratio-level agreement with PDG couplings at ~20% without RG running or model-dependent
  normalizations; improvement path points to scale-dependent mode densities ρ_i(μ).

SCATTERING BLUEPRINT
• A “topological S-matrix” is defined (scattering = transitions between topology classes via
  allowed reconnection sequences) with diagrammatic rules sketched; explicit numeric
  amplitudes remain to be computed.

DIAGNOSTIC / FALSIFIABILITY TOOLKIT
• Computable fields: misalignment angle θ4(x), reconnection barrier α_top(x) from strain S,
  link density ρ_link, and phase-gradient |∇θ4|.
• Predicted lifetime scaling: τ ∼ ℓ_f · exp(2 α_top) and environment sensitivity via θ4, ρ_link.

====================================================
5) WHAT’S MISSING OR NOT YET DEMONSTRATED
====================================================
• Scattering amplitudes: No published one-loop/multi-loop 2→2 numbers (gravity or mixed).
• Running couplings: “Topological RG” is outlined; explicit β-functions and scale calibration
  remain open.
• Fermion masses: A minimal Q-based ansatz needs fitted scales (β, γ) to hit PDG lepton
  masses → indicates this sector is incomplete under the “no free knobs” ethos.
• Peer review & adoption: Limited external scrutiny compared to mainstream frameworks.

====================================================
6) NEAR-TERM ROADMAP (FROM THE ACTION PLAN)
====================================================
• #1 Gauge/stat mech: Partition function → saddle-point metric & (g1,g2,g3); then RG flow.
• #2 Fermion mass/flavour: Construct mass operator purely from topological charge Q and
  chiral invariants; protect m_e at tree level; anomaly lifts it.
• #3 Hadrons/decays: Bound-state Hamiltonian; reconnection-barrier selection rules; OZI from
  topology.
• #4 Σ_t dynamics: Derive Euler–Lagrange equation for the resolving surface; recover light cones.
• #5 Quantum sector: Discretized filament path integral; spin-½ via ribbon statistics; 1-loop tests.
• #6 Exotics & stability: n≥4 bundle stability/no-go vs LHCb data.
• #7 Short-distance gravity: Re-derive ε from first principles (e.g., ε = ℓ_f/π), compute PN terms
  and a fixed “fifth-force” coefficient C5(T,ℓ_f).
• #8 Cosmology: Defer until 1–7 residuals are within targets.

====================================================
7) FALSIFIABILITY & EXPERIMENTAL HOOKS
====================================================
• Fifth-force/short-range gravity: Look for deviations from 1/r² below ~50 μm with a fixed,
  parameter-free coefficient C5(T,ℓ_f).
• Coupling ratios: Test g1:g2:g3 hierarchy and refined ratio predictions as topological RG is
  fleshed out.
• Decay systematics: Check lifetime scaling with α_top(S, ρ_link, |∇θ4|) across environments.
• Anomaly/EB experiments: Probe discrete winding-event interpretation of axial anomalies.

====================================================
8) BOTTOM LINE (ONE PARAGRAPH)
====================================================
SAT provides a compact, geometrically transparent ontology and a quantum measure that
promises built-in UV finiteness. It has delivered encouraging gravitational and coupling-
hierarchy signals and a concrete falsifiability toolkit. The decisive milestone now is to
compute explicit scattering amplitudes (starting with 2→2 processes) and to close the fermion
mass/running-coupling gaps without tuning. Until those are in hand and peer-reviewed, SAT
is a compelling blueprint with partial validations—not yet a drop-in replacement for QFT or
string theory.
