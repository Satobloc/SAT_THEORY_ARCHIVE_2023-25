1) What SAT is, in one paragraph
SAT (your “filament/tension” ontology) treats spacetime as a structured medium with a preferred time‑like direction, local strain, internal phase, and discrete twist. The usual metric is not fundamental but emerges from these ingredients. In that picture, clocks, light, and matter can pick up tiny, condition‑dependent phase and rate shifts from the geometry itself—not just from gravity or motion. (See the HoloJesu ACTIVATOR notes and SATxy canonical set.)

• u^μ  — preferred foliation / “arrow-of-time” field (singles out a local time direction).
• θ₄  — a scalar “misalignment/strain” of the time foliation; source of effective mass & tension.
• ψ    — internal phase; carries chiral/handed effects and couples to light and clocks.
• τ    — discrete twist/topology; provides confinement/generation structure and domain‑wall–like defects.
• g_{μν} — emergent metric built from (θ₄, u^μ), not fundamental; reduces to GR in the right limit.

Informal slogan: tension ↔ strain ↔ curvature ↔ mass ↔ (back to) tension.

4) Distinctive physical predictions (high‑level)
A) ψ‑modulated clock drift (direction‑sensitive, environment‑sensitive tiny offsets)
   • Two well‑synchronized clocks in different ψ / θ₄ environments or orientations can desynchronize at the 10⁻¹¹–10⁻¹² fractional level over long integrations.
B) Phase holonomy / “built‑in twist” tests (optics)
   • Rotate optical elements and look for residual, angle‑independent phase offsets (a geometric holonomy from ψ/τ), distinct from material asymmetries. A characteristic step‑like phase signature is predicted; null results bound wall frequency/coupling.
C) Cosmic polarization rotation (birefringence)
   • A small, correlated rotation of polarization across the sky (∼10⁻³–10⁻² rad) from a residual ψ field; shows up as uniform or patterned E→B leakage beyond ΛCDM expectations.
D) Macroscopic amplification in structured media
   • Dust/fog/aerosol flows, charged clouds, rotating cavities, and certain glasses may act as ψ–θ₄ modulators that amplify otherwise minuscule timing/phase effects.
E) Materials frontier
   • Angular thresholds and anisotropies in coherence/superconductivity (“TRON‑class” ideas) are on the exploratory list; testable but demanding experiments.

7) What would strongly support / strongly refute SAT
Support, examples
• Reproducible ψ‑driven clock offsets with sign‑flip under side swap and tight nulls on Sham/Control.
• A residual phase offset that cannot be removed by rotating/flipping material optics and persists as a geometry‑tied holonomy.
• A statistically significant, uniform polarization rotation from CMB or quasar surveys consistent with a ψ‑like field.
• Consistent anomalies in controlled aerosol/rotating‑glass experiments that scale with declared ψ/θ₄ knobs.

Refutation, examples
• A decisive demonstration that all candidate effects track conventional systematics (thermal, EM pickup, material asymmetry) under stricter controls.
• A direct observation that rules out the necessary topological sector (e.g., constraints eliminating any domain‑wall‑like holonomy within the predicted coupling range).

8) Glossary (short)
• ψ (psi): internal phase; predicts handed/CP‑odd effects in optics and timing.
• θ₄ (theta‑4): scalar strain/misalignment of the time foliation; ties to effective mass.
• u^μ: preferred local time direction (foliation field).
