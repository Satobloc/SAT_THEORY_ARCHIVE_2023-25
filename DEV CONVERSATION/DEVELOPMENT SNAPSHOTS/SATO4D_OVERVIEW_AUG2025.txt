SUMMARY:
--------

All known fields, particles, forces, and spacetime geometry emerge from the structural topology and dynamics of these filaments as intersected by a resolving 3D wavefront (Σₜ) interpreted as the "present".

The theory is defined entirely within the static 4D "block universe" formalism (Interpretive Mode 1) with controlled speculative extensions permitted only under explicit modular isolation.

FOUNDATIONAL ASSUMPTIONS:
-------------------------
- The universe is a 4D smooth differentiable manifold M with no prior fields or metric.
- Physical reality is composed of 1D filaments γ: ℝ → M.
- A propagating time-resolving surface Σₜ intersects the block to generate observable 3+1 phenomena.
- No prior dynamics are assumed; motion, mass, and causality are emergent from geometry.
