
SAT Predictions and Retrodictions — Summary List
================================================

1. Particle Masses
------------------
- Formula: m_i = m0 / Q_i, with m0 ~ Planck mass.
- Retrodiction: Proton/electron mass ratio predicted ~10^4 vs observed ~1836.
  • Tolerance: within factor ~5 (good at ratio level).
- Absolute masses: Predicted ~30 orders of magnitude too small.
  • Tolerance: fails badly on absolute scale, needs rescaling of Q values.

2. Force Strength Hierarchy (Gravity vs EM)
-------------------------------------------
- Prediction: Q_em/Q_g ≈ 2 × 10^19 required to reproduce observed force ratio.
- Retrodiction: If Q_em and Q_g are of similar order, the ratio is missed by ~19 orders.
  • Tolerance: must reproduce ratio within order of magnitude (current draft needs adjustment).

3. Fine-Structure Constant α_EM
-------------------------------
- Target: α = 1/137.
- Current sketch: unclear mapping from topological numbers to α.
- Tolerance: Must reproduce to within <1% to be viable (experimentally known to parts in 10^10).

4. Strong Coupling α_s and Running Couplings
--------------------------------------------
- Prediction: Couplings should show logarithmic running with scale.
- Retrodiction: Needs to reproduce known QCD running (LEP/LHC data).
- Tolerance: Must match observed running within experimental uncertainties (few %).

5. Vacuum Energy Density (Dark Energy)
--------------------------------------
- Naïve Planck estimate: 10^113 J/m^3.
- Observed: ~6 × 10^-10 J/m^3.
- Required suppression factor: ~10^122.
- Tolerance: Needs exact cancellation at 122 orders of magnitude (major challenge).

6. Black Hole Entropy
---------------------
- Prediction: Each Planck area patch has ~1.3 filament microstates.
- Retrodiction: Correctly reproduces S = A/4L_P^2 with coefficient 1/4.
  • Tolerance: Exact agreement (within theoretical derivation).

7. Hawking Radiation
--------------------
- Prediction: Emission arises from filament reconnections rather than vacuum pair creation.
- Retrodiction: Spectrum must match Hawking’s thermal spectrum to high accuracy.
  • Tolerance: Must reproduce within observational tests of black hole evaporation (not yet measured).

8. Supersymmetry
----------------
- Prediction: Reinterpreted as dual vibration modes of filaments, not new particles.
- Retrodiction: No low-energy SUSY partners expected.
  • Tolerance: Compatible with LHC non-detection (good consistency).

9. Exotic Hadrons / Topological States
--------------------------------------
- Prediction: New hadrons correspond to higher-order filament knots/links.
- Retrodiction: Already explains existence of tetraquarks/pentaquarks qualitatively.
  • Tolerance: Needs quantitative mass predictions within ~10% to be credible.

10. Thermodynamics
------------------
- Prediction: Entropy and temperature emerge from filament counting.
- Retrodiction: Should reproduce standard thermodynamic relations (E, S, T) at macroscales.
  • Tolerance: Must match within experimental thermodynamic precision (~10^-5 relative).

11. Cosmology
-------------
- Prediction: Large-scale structure seeded by statistical fluctuations of filament networks.
- Retrodiction: Should match CMB spectrum (anisotropies at ~10^-5 level).
  • Tolerance: Requires precise match to Planck satellite data.

12. Gravitational Waves
-----------------------
- Prediction: Waves correspond to collective oscillations of filament bundles.
- Retrodiction: Must match LIGO/Virgo waveforms (binary mergers).
  • Tolerance: Within <1% phase/amplitude deviation over thousands of cycles.

================================================
Overall Assessment
------------------
- Strong matches: black-hole entropy, proton/electron mass ratio (roughly), SUSY reinterpretation.
- Weak matches: absolute particle mass scale, fine-structure constant, vacuum energy.
- Yet to be tested: exotic hadrons (quantitatively), CMB precision fit, gravitational wave phase-accuracy.
