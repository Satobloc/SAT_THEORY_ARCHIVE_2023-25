Overview
--------

The key idea is that what we observe as particles, forces, and curvature are all projections or statistical patterns of filament dynamics — including linking, twisting, density, strain, and phase coherence — within a fully four-dimensional geometry.

Core Principles
---------------
1. All matter corresponds to geometric structure: 4D worldtubes of vibrating filaments.
2. Binding arises from topological linking (e.g., Hopf, Borromean structures).
3. Mass is topological strain: a result of angular mismatch or resistance in 4D alignment.
4. Fields (EM, QCD, Higgs, etc.) emerge from coherent oscillations or configurations.
5. Quantum behavior arises statistically from large-scale filament configurations.
6. Spacetime curvature is emergent: encoded in filament density, orientation, and shear.
7. All classical and quantum dynamics are expressions of 4D shape, not 3D evolution.
8. The universe is a 4D object. We experience 3D slices due to our embedded trajectory.
