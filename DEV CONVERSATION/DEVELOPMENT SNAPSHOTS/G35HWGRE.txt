SAT.4D — Structural Overview (August 2025)

=================================================
1. DIRECTIVES & PURPOSE
-------------------------------------------------
- SAT.O is a structural, geometric, and topological framework built on a 4D differentiable manifold M populated by 1D filaments.
- No metric, field, or external dynamics are assumed a priori.
- All observables (mass, charge, curvature, interaction) emerge from filament geometry and its intersection with a 3D resolving surface Σ_t.
- Core principle: all quantities must be expressible in SAT-native geometric and topological terms, with falsifiability conditions.

References: SAT_DIRECTIVES.txt [July 2025]
