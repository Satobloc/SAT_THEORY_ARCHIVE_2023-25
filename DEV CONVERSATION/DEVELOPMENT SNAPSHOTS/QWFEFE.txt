SAT.4D Predictions and Retrodictions (August 2025)
=================================================

This file lists all current predictions (forward-looking) and retrodictions (already-confirmed features of known physics) implied by the SAT.4D framework, with tolerances where possible.

-------------------------------------------------
1. Retrodictions (Explaining Known Physics)
-------------------------------------------------
- Gauge Groups:
  • U(1), SU(2), SU(3) emerge naturally from filament phase-locking (O3).
  • Retrodiction: correct Standard Model gauge structure.
  • Tolerance: exact; categorical rather than numerical.

- Coupling Ratios:
  • Relations g_SU2/g_U1 ≈ sqrt(rho_wind / rho_link).
  • Retrodiction: hierarchy of couplings (g_U1 > g_SU2 > g_SU3).
  • Tolerance: order-of-magnitude agreement at unification scale; ±20%.

- Mass Suppression:
  • Effective mass m_eff = m0 / Q.
  • Retrodiction: neutrino/electron mass hierarchy consistent with Q=1 leptonic cycle.
  • Tolerance: factor-of-few accuracy (current calibration within ~5–10% for e, μ, π).

- Stability Bounds:
  • Only n=1,2,3 composite states stable (O4).
  • Retrodiction: stability of leptons, mesons, baryons; absence of stable tetraquarks/pentaquarks.
  • Tolerance: categorical falsifiability—any stable n≥4 would refute.

- Emergent Gravity:
  • Metric and curvature derived statistically (O2).
  • Retrodiction: Einstein-Hilbert action form recovered.
  • Tolerance: exact at effective field theory level.

-------------------------------------------------
2. Predictions (Testable Forward-Looking)
-------------------------------------------------
- Leptonic Excitations (O9):
  • Predicted light states at Q>3, N=0–4 (m ≈ 0.2–0.5 GeV).
  • Prediction: if not observed, implies automatic topological suppression.
  • Tolerance: ±10 MeV per state in spectrum fits.

- Hadronic Ground States (O10):
  • Q=3 → ρ(770): 0.799 GeV predicted vs. 0.775 GeV observed (Δ≈3%).
  • Q=4 → K*(892): 0.863 GeV predicted vs. 0.892 GeV observed (Δ≈3%).
  • Q=6 → φ(1020): 1.025 GeV predicted vs. 1.019 GeV observed (Δ≈0.6%).
  • Tolerance: ±5%.

- Hadronic Radial Excitations (O10):
  • ρ(1450): predicted 1.450 GeV vs. 1.450 GeV observed (exact by fit).
  • K*(1410): predicted 1.487 GeV vs. 1.414 GeV observed (Δ≈5%).
  • φ(1680): predicted 1.586 GeV vs. 1.680 GeV observed (Δ≈6%).
  • Tolerance: ±5–7%.

- Higher Hadronic States:
  • Q=7–10 → predicted vector states at 1.10–1.35 GeV range.
  • Experimental window: dense vector/baryon resonance region.
  • Tolerance: ±50 MeV.

- Coupling Evolution:
  • Running couplings encoded in density ratios rho_wind, rho_link, rho_triplet.
  • Prediction: testable at LHC/ILC scales once mapping is numerically fixed.
  • Tolerance: ±10% after calibration.

- Quantum Extensions:
  • Curvature-dependent mass shift: m_eff(R) = m0/Q + αR.
  • Prediction: small shifts in dispersion relations in strong curvature (neutron stars, cosmology).
  • Tolerance: order-of-magnitude estimate; currently unconstrained.

- Entropic Proper Time:
  • Prediction: dτ = S(x) dφ measurable as drift in emergent clocks at high strain.
  • Tolerance: qualitative until experimental strain data is mapped.

-------------------------------------------------
3. Falsifiability Criteria
-------------------------------------------------
- Stable n≥4 composite state → falsifies O4.
- Absence of expected Q=3–6 hadron matches → falsifies O10 fit.
- Incompatibility of coupling hierarchy → falsifies O5.
- No emergence of Einstein-Hilbert action from O2 statistics → falsifies O2.
